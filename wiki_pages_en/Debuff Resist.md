# Debuff Resist

Debuff Resist is a stat which reduces the length of time negative effects affect a hero.

This stat is gained in the form of a percentage. As an example, "30% Debuff Resist" will reduce the duration of a 10 second debuff by 3 seconds.

Debuff Resist can be gained by purchasing specific items at the Shop.
