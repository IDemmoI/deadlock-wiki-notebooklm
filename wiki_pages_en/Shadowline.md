## Overview

[edit | edit source]

Not much is known about *Shadowline*. It existed at some point in the transition from *Neon Prime* to *Deadlock*, in 2022-2023. The web domain *playshadowline.com* and twitter account *@PlayShadowline* were registered in November 2023. It is assumed to be the first version to use the 20th Century American occult setting.

In this version, the ziplines were called "shadowlines",[1] and Astral Gates were "shadowline nexus". The shadowlines were based on ley lines.

## Mentions

[edit | edit source]

- The Shopkeeper narrated an old version of the tutorial that refers to the game as *Shadowline.*[2][1]
- Kelvin may reference a shadowline when upgrading his Ice Path.[3]
- In Viscous' background, the Bermuda Triangle Astral Gate is described as a "shadowline nexus".

## Caldera_trooper

[edit | edit source]

**Caldera_trooper** was a map found in early builds of *Deadlock* that presumably started development during the *Shadowline* period. It had a similar layout to *Neon Prime's* map, but with new lighting and assets from what would later become the Cursed Apple. Caldera_trooper was last compiled in July 2023.
