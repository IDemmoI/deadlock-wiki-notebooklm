The daughter of a rare book dealer, Paige grew up in a loving home that prioritized kindness, magic, and storytelling.  But while Paige took naturally to her parents' values, her little brother Bryce did not.

A rebellious teen, Bryce resented not only his family’s saccharine attitude, but also their refusal to let him dabble in magics that they deemed too dangerous.  Paige tried everything she could think of to keep him out of trouble, but like most teenagers, the more his big sister tried to tell him what was best for him, the more he turned away.

Paige doesn't know what Bryce did to get kicked out of the house...just that she came home from the library to find her brother gone and her parents crying.

Days turned to weeks, to months, to years.  Paige grew to become a talented bibliomancer, making a living as a storyteller reading at libraries across the five boroughs; and while her parents have remained silent about what happened with Bryce, Paige's obsession with finding him has only grown.  She will discover what happened to her brother... and no man or god is going to stand in her way.

## Overview

[edit | edit source]

Paige's motivation in the ritual is to find and save her brother, Bryce.[1][2]

### Biography

[edit | edit source]

Paige reads books to kindergarteners.[3][4] She loved showing off  Bookwyrm at the library, but was forced to stop after the dragon burnt some books.[5][6]

### Personality

[edit | edit source]

Paige loves reading all kinds of books, having set times of day for different types of books.[7] Aside from real-world novels, Paige has read books on  Kelvin's adventures and the Baxter Society.[8][9]

### Information

[edit | edit source]

Paige had a diary in high school where she wrote about byronic heroes.[10]

### Literature References

[edit | edit source]

Paige makes several references to real life literature and authors. These include:

- Various references to Arthurian Legend.[11][12]
- *A Midsummer Night's Dream,* written by William Shakespeare and performed in 1595-1596.[11]
- *Henry V,* written by Shakespeare and performed in 1599-1600.[13]
- *Othello,* written by William Shakespeare and performed in 1601-1604.[14]
- *Pride and Prejudice,* written by Jane Austen and published in 1813.[15][16]
- *Wuthering Heights,* written by Emily Brontë and published in 1847.[11][16][15]
- *The Picture of Dorian Gray,* written by Oscar Wilde and published in 1891.[17]
- *Sherlock Holmes,* specifically *The Adventure of the Empty House,* written by Arthur Conan Doyle and published in 1903.[18]
- *The Wind in the Willows,* written by Kenneth Grahame and published in 1908.[19]
- *Hercule Poirot,* a fictional detective from a series written by Agatha Christie.[20]
- *Of Mice and Men,* written by John Steinbeck and published in 1937.[21]
- *The Lion, The Witch and The Wardrobe,* written by C. S. Lewis and published in 1950.[22]

## Character Interactions

[edit | edit source]

- Paige likes heroic men[32][33] and has a crush on  Mirage,[34]  Shiv[35] and  Victor.[36][37]
- Paige admires  Mirage and says he "stepped out of every romance novel ever written".[38][39] She thinks Mirage is better than  Shiv.[40]
- Paige wants to give Billy and Viscous a hug.[41][42] Celeste wants to give Paige a hug.[43]
- Paige compares Billy to Nick Bottom from *A Midsummer Night's Dream*[44] and Mr. Tumnus from *The Chronicles of Narnia*.[45]
- Paige calls Lash a "dimestore Mr. Collins".[46]
- Paige loves animals like Mo and Ava.[47][48] She believes that Calico "can't be evil" because she has a cat.[49]
- Paige thinks Holliday shouldn't have a gun due to her mental state.[50][51]
- Vyper has met Paige's brother and says he was "more fun".[52] Bryce told her that Paige is "wound up tight".[53]
- The Doorman likes Paige's taste in books and invites her to host a storytelling event at the Baroness Hotel.[54][55]
- Yamato likes Paige, likening her to a "puppy that pisses on the rug: stupid, yet strangely endearing".[56][57]
- Infernus and the Shopkeeper are familiar with Paige's family.[58][59]
- Lash respects Paige for going to college.[60]
- Paradox is interested in stealing Paige's rare books.[61]
