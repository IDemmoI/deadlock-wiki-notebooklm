## Weapon

[edit | edit source]

Tokamak has an unique gimmick for their weapon: Tokamak's ammo bar takes the form of a heat gauge which rises the longer the player fires for and lowers when Tokamak is not firing their weapon. Past a certain point, Tokamak's weapon will begin to overheat, granting increased firing speed. However, the player must be careful, because if Tokamak overheats, they will be unable to fire their gun for a few seconds.

Due to the fact that Tokamak is unfinished, the heat gauge will stay on the HUD even if the player switches to another hero.

## Trivia

[edit | edit source]

- Tokamak's model was a modified version of  Bebop's model.
- Fathom's second ability, **Breach**, is a combination of two abilities Tokamak had: Functionally, it is identical to Tokamak's **Dying Star** and its name was taken from a removed ability for Tokamak also called **Breach**.
- Raven's ultimate ability, **Revelation**, is also functionally similar to Tomamak's **Blinding Radiance**: Enemies are afflicted with debuffs and damage if they look towards them while their ability is active.
