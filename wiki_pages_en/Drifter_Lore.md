## Overview

[edit | edit source]

Unlike the other heroes, Drifter isn't participating in the ritual for a wish, but instead for the carnage.[1] He views the wish as a "bonus".[2]

### Biography

[edit | edit source]

The Drifter has lived for centuries.[3] His age is unknown, but he refers to New York as "New Amsterdam",[4] which implies he's been around at least since 1665.[5] He also implies that he knew Doc Holliday from the 19th century.[6]

Drifter speaks in a Cajun accent from Louisiana, indicating that he may be associated with 17th century Acadian French immigrants.[7]

Drifter has been hunted by the Baxter Society, the Swords of Saint Benedict, and Silver.[8][9][10] The OSIC aren't after Drifter.[11]

### Personality

[edit | edit source]

Drifter loves murdering people. He doesn't enjoy killing  Bebop because he's a machine,[12]  Sinclair because they're already dead,[13] and  Victor because he's just putting him out of his misery.[14]

Drifter seems to be scared of fire, and expresses relief if he doesn't have to fight  Infernus.[15]

### Information

[edit | edit source]

Drifter is not the Troubadour.[16][17] He has never met him before, and only knows him by reputation.[18]

Drifter likes to steal his victim's coats.[19][20]

## Character Relationships

[edit | edit source]

### Grey Talon

[edit | edit source]

Drifter and Grey Talon have a long history with each other.

Drifter refers to Grey Talon by his first name "Wesley".[21]

While Grey Talon despises Drifter,[22][23] Drifter holds Talon in high regards.[24][25][26]

Drifter enjoys tormenting Talon, seemingly by hurting his loved ones.[27][28]

There was a particularly brutal interaction between the two in Oklahoma.[29] Vyper mentions that Drifter is a "legend" in Oklahoma.[30]

### Shiv

[edit | edit source]

Drifter knew Shiv from before he joined the Baxter Society.[31]

If Drifter kills Shiv, he calls him a "disappointment" and mentions that killing him feels "Shakespearean".[32][33][34] He also mentions that, had Shiv not "put himself in a muzzle, he'd be worried".[35]

When hunting Shiv, Drifter refers to himself as an "old friend".[36]

How Shiv and Drifter met each other is unknown.

### Oathkeeper

[edit | edit source]

Drifter and Oathkeeper are old friends.[37]

Drifter can't believe  Lady Geist could lock away "someone as fun as Oathkeeper".[38][39]

## Character Interactions

[edit | edit source]

- Drifter thinks Mina is a "pathetic excuse for a vampire".[40] Mina is terrified of Drifter.[41][42]
- Drifter warns Holliday to let go of her obsession with the Troubadour before the Troubadour finds out about her chasing him.[43]
- Drifter has killed Venators before,[44] though he had higher expectations for the playable Venator.[45] If they're on the same team, Venator promises to kill Drifter after the ritual.[46]
- Drifter has high expectations for Seven,[47][48] but thinks he still has too much humanity left in him.[49]
- Drifter calls  Mirage "handsome". [50]
- The Doorman has met Drifter before.[51]
- Paradox is annoyed that Drifter has nothing worth stealing.[52]
