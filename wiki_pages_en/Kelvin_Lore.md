A famed adventuring scientist, Kelvin made it his life's mission to explore and understand the unknowable.  So naturally when rumors surfaced regarding an 8th astral gate hidden in the arctic, Kelvin couldn't build an expedition team fast enough.  Assembling the brightest minds of his generation, Kelvin and his team set forth with much fanfare.

They never returned.

A year and a half later the frozen body of Kelvin was found by a fishing trawler, his corpse clutching a rock etched with glowing runes.  The crew chipped away at the ice, looking to get a closer look at the body and were shocked when Kelvin opened his eyes.  They checked his vitals... there was no pulse.  They sought to bandage his open wounds... there was no blood.  And yet, impossibly, Kelvin was "alive".

Having no memory of what happened on the expedition, Kelvin is looking for answers.

## Overview

[edit | edit source]

Kelvin's motivation and wish in the ritual is to rediscover what happened during the expedition to the Arctic.[1][2]

### Biography

[edit | edit source]

As a famous explorer, Kelvin has written successful books about his adventures.[3][4] One of his past expeditions was located at Ixia.[5]

Kelvin is consistently called an "Ice Wight" due to his undead state.[6] Kelvin believes the correct term is "Revenant", although he's unsure if there's really a difference.[7]

Kelvin's ice abilities were created with a mix of science and magic.[8][9] They seem unrelated to his glowing rock or undead condition, although he claims to be the only person able to safely use Arctic Beam.[10][11] This is due to the fact that Kelvin doesn't get cold anymore.[12]

### Personality

[edit | edit source]

Kelvin exhibits a boastful personality.[13][14] He seems to have a reputation for being a little judgmental.[15]

Kelvin allegedly wrestles with a lot of guilt about the expedition.[16] He's been meeting with family members of his crewmates who never returned from it.[17] Despite the guilt, Kelvin remains optimistic about the ritual and his future.[18][19]

Kelvin views his undead state as a gift.[20]

### Information

[edit | edit source]

The OSIC want to ask Kelvin questions about his undead condition.[21]

Kelvin hasn't taken off his parka since the expedition just in case the glowing rock strapped to his body is the only thing keeping him alive.[22]

Kelvin likes to drink single malt Scotch.[23][24] He used to frequent Jezebel's before his Arctic expedition.[25]

Kelvin's parka smells.[26][27]

## Character Relationships

[edit | edit source]

### Ettrick

[edit | edit source]

One of Kelvin's old friends was someone named "Ettrick", who Kelvin pushed away due to Ettrick's ambitions.[28] At the fundraiser for Kelvin's Arctic expedition, Ettrick "made a scene". When Kelvin returned from the expedition, he tried to find Ettrick but couldn't.[29] Kelvin seems remorseful about Ettrick, and believes that "if someone's going to kill him, it's going to be Ettrick".[30][31]

### Seven

[edit | edit source]

Seven has a one-sided hatred towards Kelvin for unknown reasons.[32][33][34]

Kelvin thinks Seven "seems familiar".[35] When being healed by Kelvin, Seven is reminded of "old times".[36]

Both Seven and Kelvin have an interest on researching Astral Gates.[37][38]

Seven believes Kelvin has a bigger ego than  Lash.[39]

A likely possibility is that Seven is Kelvin's old friend Ettrick. This is supported by one of Seven's lines to Kelvin that mirrors one of Kelvin's lines about Ettrick.[40][41] Kelvin pushed Ettrick away due to his "ambition", which aligns with Seven's ambition to achieve apotheosis.[42] Seven also believes that mentors "use the young to achieve glories they could not achieve on their own", possibly referring to Kelvin.[43]

### Dynamo

[edit | edit source]

Dynamo and Kelvin are friends. They last saw each other at the Black Château, before Kelvin disappeared.[44]

Dynamo is a fan of Kelvin's books.[45]

Dynamo sometimes refers to Kelvin as "Kevin".[46]

### Lady Geist

[edit | edit source]

Kelvin's Arctic expedition was funded by Lady Geist.[47][48] Lady Geist takes great offense if Kelvin is on the enemy team.[49][50]

### Victor

[edit | edit source]

Kelvin and Victor promised each other that they'd stick together, as they're both walking corpses.[51][52]

## Character Interactions

[edit | edit source]

- Kelvin understands why  Grey Talon and  Vindicta are consumed by rage and is sorry for them, but insists that their rage won't grant them peace.[53][54][55][56]
- Kelvin admires  Mo & Krill's friendship.[57]
- Kelvin is one of  Paradox's targets.[58] According to Paradox, the Paradox Organisation holds Kelvin in high regards.[59]
- Apollo,[60]  Dynamo,[61] Graves,[62] Ms. Shelly,[63]  Mo & Krill and  Ivy's brother (Manny) have read stories about Kelvin's adventures.[64]

- Paige has also read Kelvin's books and idolises him.[65][66]
- McGinnis consistently refers to Kelvin as "Kevin".[67][68][69]
- Graves believes her mentor would love to get her hands on Kelvin's stone.[70] She also thinks that might be able to help Kelvin with his undead condition.[71]
- According to Rem, Kelvin smells like "nice s'mores".[72] This contrasts Seven's scent of "evil s'mores".[73]
