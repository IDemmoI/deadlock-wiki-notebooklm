**Graf** is an unreleased hero in *Deadlock*.

In the game files, there's a reference to a model called "graffiti_girl" under Graf's name, indicating that she could be a graffiti artist.

## Abilities

[edit | edit source]

Graf currently has no working abilities.

## Weapon

[edit | edit source]

Graf uses the default gen-man gun. However, their gun is listed as "burst fire" and "medium range" in-game.

Graf's weapon has the exact same stats as Fortuna's weapon.

## Trivia

[edit | edit source]

- Graf was first revealed through Victor and Mina's voice lines in the Shop Rework Update (May 8th 2025)[1][2]. A playable, although minimalistic, version of Graf was added to the files in the Six New Heroes Update (August 18th 2025).
- Graf has the lowest health in the game at 480hp with no boons (100hp less than Mina), and 1472 with max boons (8hp less than Mina).
- Besides "graffiti", the name "Graf" could also mean an earl/count of German nobility, or a slang for "paragraph".
