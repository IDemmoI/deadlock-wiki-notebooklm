## Overview

[edit | edit source]

When the player triggers Cheat Death, a visual effect of a scythe and a contract that reads *"Savor your moments, cheat the end, but remember - Death always collects what is owed"* appears above the player's head.

## Notable Interactions

[edit | edit source]

- The Rejuvenator credit is triggered *after* Cheat Death.
- Victor's  Shocking Reanimation is triggered *before* Cheat Death.

- An exception is for  Shiv's  Killing Blow, which will trigger Shocking Reanimation and Cheat Death at the same time. This is likely unintentional.

## Trivia

[edit | edit source]

- Cheat Death has the same name and similar effect to one of the abilities of the unreleased hero Rutger.
- Cheat Death's icon has been redesigned multiple times: Originally it showed a hand holding 5 aces in a poker game against Death, then as a person kicking the door of a coffin, and currently is a hand holding 5 aces including a skull suit.
