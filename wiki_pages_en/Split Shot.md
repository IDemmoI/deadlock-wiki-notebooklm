**Split Shot** is a Tier 2  Weapon Item that can be purchased from the Shop for  **1,600**.

## Contents

- 1 Notes
- 2 Notable interactions
- 3 Update history
- 4 Navigation

## Notes

[edit | edit source]

- Multishots can hit units as well, but they do not grant stacks.
- Each shot is separated by approximately 5°, leading to a total of approximately 20° spread between the furthest two shots.

## Notable interactions

[edit | edit source]

- Like  Armor Piercing Rounds, Split Shot's bullets can penetrate  Kelvin's  Frozen Shelter and  McGinnis'  Spectral Wall. This effect is probably unintended.
- For  Celeste, it splits into 3 shots at 70° spread.
- Split Shot affects  Grey Talon's  Rain of Arrows, allowing him to fire 25 arrows at once.
- Split Shot affects  Paradox's  Kinetic Carbine, allowing her to fire 5 Kinetic Carbine shots at once. However, it will only affect the first enemy hit.
- Paige and  The Doorman can gain stacks by hitting multiple players with the same shot.
- Split Shot has no effect on  Silver (Transformed).
