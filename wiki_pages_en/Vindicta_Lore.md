A victim of John Hathorne during the Salem Witch Trials, Vindicta got a second chance of life when her spirit was brought back to the mortal plane during the first Maelstrom (the event that introduced the supernatural to the public at large).

Waging a one ghost war against "The Friends of Humanity", a neo inquisition formed by Hathorne's descendants; Vindicta looks to keep the supernatural safe from Hathorne's ilk, and she isn't afraid to stack as many bodies as needed to do it.

## Overview

[edit | edit source]

Vindicta's motivation in the ritual is to wipe out John Hathorne's bloodline and the Friends of Humanity.[1][2][3][4] Her main goal seems to be getting revenge on Hathorne's bloodline, with the destruction of the Friends of Humanity being a side benefit.[5]

### Biography

[edit | edit source]

Vindicta says she uses her given name.[6] Puritans in the 17th Century were known to give unusual names to their children, as a way of honoring their religion.[7][8]

Vindicta says she was a child who was "unjustly condemned" during the Salem Witch Trials.[9] John Hathorne, who's mentioned in Vindicta's background, was known in the actual Salem Witch Trials for questioning accusers and the accused in trial, often leaving the accused guilty.[10]

Vindicta has been dead for roughly 200 years[11] before returning to life during the first Maelstrom (50 years before the events of the game).[12]

Vindicta's main target is the current leader of the Friends of Humanity, also named John Hathorne, who is a descendant of the original Hathorne who killed her.[13][14] Vindicta has killed John Hathorne's brother while he was on a speedboat,[15] and assassinated someone else during the Boston Marathon.[16]

Vindicta's exploits have been told about in Ixia. According to  Apollo, Vindicta is called *"Hasha Vogali",* which is Ixian for "Death's Whisper".[17]

### Personality

[edit | edit source]

Vindicta says she's not a witch because, in her eyes, she doesn't have wants or dreams: only a lust for revenge.[18]

After returning from the dead, Vindicta says she was "lost" for a while before finding her purpose of revenge.[19] Despite being consumed by revenge, Vindicta ultimately sees her actions as her "saving life".[20][21]

### Grey Talon

[edit | edit source]

Vindicta and Grey Talon have hunted the Friends of Humanity together and regard each other as friends.[22][23][24]

Vindicta calls Grey Talon by his first name and believes that, if her father was like Grey Talon, she would never have died during the Salem Witch Trials.[25]

## Character Interactions

[edit | edit source]

- Vindicta is referred to as Goody Proctor by  Mo & Krill.[26]
- Vindicta believes  Ivy is naive and warns her that, if she doesn't smarten up soon, someone she cares about will be hurt.[27]
- Haze warns Vindicta to "quit while she's ahead", because murdering John Hathorne will "galvanize" the Friends of Humanity.[28] She calls Vindicta a "domestic terrorist".[29]
- Apollo commends Vindicta for her war against the Friends of Humanity, although he dislikes her dishonorable tactics.[30][31]
- Graves wishes she could help Vindicta with her problems.[32][33]
- Abrams,[34]  Kelvin,[35]  Paige[36] and  Warden[37] are sympathetic to Vindicta's cause.
- Abrams,[38]  Billy,[39]  Holliday,[40]  Mina,[41]  Wraith[42] and  Yamato[43] point out that Vindicta doesn't wear shoes.

- Rem likes that Vindicta also doesn't wear shoes.[44]
