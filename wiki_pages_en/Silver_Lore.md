## Overview

[edit | edit source]

Silver's goal in the ritual is to get money and sort her life out.[1][2][3][4]

### Biography

[edit | edit source]

Silver has been a werewolf for as long as she can remember.[5]

Silver claims to have her Lycanthropy under control.[6] However, Silver seems unable to control her transformation due to it triggering automatically in-game without her compliance.[7]

Silver tries to take bounties in alive, although her methods usually end up killing them.[8] She's brought  Vyper into custody on several occasions and is after a bounty on The Troubadour.[9][10]

### Personality

[edit | edit source]

Silver hates her life and is aware of her self-destructive tendencies.[11][12] While she doesn't love being a bounty hunter, she believes she isn't good at anything else and would end up homeless if she didn't do a job that paid well.[13][14]

Silver has a habit of spending all of her money on rent or alcohol.[15]

Silver is paranoid that her teammates will steal her bounties.[16][17]

### Information

[edit | edit source]

Silver has an unnamed ex-partner who accused her of pushing people away.[18]

Silver once got kicked out of the Baroness Hotel during brunch.[19][20]

## Character Relationships

[edit | edit source]

### Shiv

[edit | edit source]

Silver loathes Shiv with a passion and refuses to believe he could change from his criminal past.[21][22] She believes he deserves a fate worse than death.[23]

How Silver and Shiv met each other and why Silver hates Shiv is currently unknown.

An unfinished conversation with  Grey Talon hints at Silver in disbelief that Grey Talon would support Shiv.[24]

### Abrams

[edit | edit source]

Silver is old friends with Abrams, referring to him as "Abe".[25]

According to Silver, they used to make "bad decisions" before Abrams became "boring".[26]

### Infernus and Hank

[edit | edit source]

As a heavy drinker, Silver frequents Jezebel's and is friends with Hank and Infernus.[27][28]

Silver refers to Infernus as "Fern".[29]

### Vyper

[edit | edit source]

Silver has hunted and caught Vyper so many times that Vyper's use of  Petrifying Bola comes from Silver's  Entangling Bola.[30][31]

### Mo & Krill

[edit | edit source]

Mo and Krill have worked with Silver in the past and have a good working relationship with her.[32]

While on the other team, they express concern over "how desperate" Silver has gotten and wish they could've helped her.[33]

## Character Interactions

[edit | edit source]

- Silver plays in Wraith's weekly poker games.[34]
- Silver is implied to have been hunting Drifter for a while and hates him.[35][36] Silver is also after a bounty on Calico.[37]
- Silver and Venator get along with each other, planning to go drink whiskey together after the ritual.[38][39]
- Celeste offers to have a drink with Silver after the ritual.[40]
- According to Rem, Silver's hair smells like Bourbon.[41]
