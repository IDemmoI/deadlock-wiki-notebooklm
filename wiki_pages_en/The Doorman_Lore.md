## Overview

[edit | edit source]

The Doorman's motivation in the ritual is to see the Patrons, since he hasn't seen them in a long time.[1][2] He doesn't seem to have a wish planned as he intends to return to the Baroness after the ritual,[3][4] although the Patrons goad him to wish back his old power.[5]

### Biography

[edit | edit source]

The Doorman is referred to as a fledgling god (by himself),[6] a Patron (by himself)[7] and a cosmic being.[8] He often refers to the other heroes as "mortals".[9][10]

"The Doorman" is not the Doorman's real name, but one he uses to "keep himself grounded" to remind himself that he's here to serve, not to rule.[11]

There are lines which imply that, a long time ago, Doorman used to slaughter humans, expressing glee at the chance to kill again and be feared during the ritual.[12][13] The true nature of the Doorman is unknown to most of the cast.[14]

### Personality

[edit | edit source]

The Doorman is polite and respectful when talking to the other heroes.

### Information

[edit | edit source]

The Doorman likes working at the Baroness since he gets to observe humanity.[15]

The OSIC seem to be aware of the Doorman's true nature, as they consider Doorman participating in the ritual as "overstepping".[16]

The Doorman claims to be able to see the future.[17]

## Character Relationships

[edit | edit source]

### The Patrons

[edit | edit source]

The Doorman calls the Patrons "old friends" and refers to them as City Mother and Amber King.[18][19]

Both Patrons are familiar with the Doorman, expressing surprise and joy at seeing him fighting on their behalf.[20][21] They respect The Doorman and view his murderous side as "who he was meant to be".[22]

## Character Interactions

[edit | edit source]

- The Doorman is extremely knowledgeable about every other hero in the game, tormenting them while they're trapped in the Baroness.[23] He doesn't seem to know what  Warden wants,[24] which could mean he's telepathic like the Patrons as opposed to being omniscient, since they can't read Warden's desires either.[25]
- The Doorman respects  Infernus since they both work in the service industry and is apologetic when killing him.[26][27]
- The Doorman thinks  Wraith is a "delight" and is grateful towards her since she's a generous tipper.[28][29]
- The Doorman thinks  Lady Geist is rude and doesn't expect any tips from her.[30][31]
- The Doorman is actively keeping  Paradox out of the Baroness.[32]
- The Doorman has kicked  Silver and  Vyper out of the Baroness.[33][34]
- Both Patrons are familiar with the Doorman, expressing surprise and joy at seeing him fighting on their behalf.[20][21] They respect The Doorman and view his murderous side as "who he was meant to be".[22]
- Mina and  Lady Geist don't like talking to The Doorman since they view him as just "the help".[35][36]
- Venator recognises that Doorman is more ancient and powerful than he lets on.[37][38]
- Seven knows that Doorman isn't normal and wants to uncover his secrets.[39][40]
