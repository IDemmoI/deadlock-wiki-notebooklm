## Weapon

[edit | edit source]

Fortuna uses the default gen-man gun. Their weapon is listed as "rapid fire" and "medium range" in-game.

Instead of regular bullets, Fortuna fires green orbs that have slight homing capabilities.

## Trivia

[edit | edit source]

- Fortuna was first revealed through  Victor and  Mina's voice lines in the Shop Rework Update (May 8th 2025)[1][2]. A playable, although minimalistic, version of Fortuna was added to the files in the Six New Heroes Update (August 18th 2025).
- Rem's weapon projectiles are similar to Fortuna's, though they have a smaller spread.
