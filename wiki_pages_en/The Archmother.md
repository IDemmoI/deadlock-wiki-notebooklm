**The Archmother** is a Patron, as well as the name of her Team. Her followers are **The Archmother's Disciples**. The Hidden King is her rival.

The Archmother's herald is voiced by Debra Wilson.[1]

## Overview

[edit | edit source]

The Archmother wears a broken mask that resembles the head of the Statue of Liberty, her form bound to a crystalline effigy burning with blue flames. She's characterized by the color blue. Her base is a museum with a Neoclassic aesthetic. Her side of the map has upper-class white brick buildings and is labeled "Uptown".

The Archmother does not speak to the heroes directly. Instead, a herald speaks on her behalf.[2][3]

Akin to the Hidden King, the Archmother's goals are unknown. According to The Doorman, the Archmother has wanted to be summoned for "centuries".[4] Warden and his family believe the Patrons will "herald the end of humanity" if left unchecked.[5] It's implied that the Archmother wants to "usher in her new era".[6][7]

The Doorman refers to the Archmother as **City Mother**.[8] The bank behind Archmother's Yellow Walker is called "City Mother Bank".

The Hidden King's herald calls the Archmother a "tyrant".[9]

## Trivia

[edit | edit source]

- The Archmother was originally named **The Sapphire Flame** prior to the Old Gods, New Blood update. She also referred to herself as **Whisper in the Wind** and **Ambition's Spark** in an unused voice line.[10] This is referenced in a Warden unselect line.[11]

- A proposed rename to the Archmother was **The Guided Hand**.[12]
