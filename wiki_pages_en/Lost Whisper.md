# Lost Whisper

“

Sometimes I think "Man, it would be so fun to break out of Lost Whisper", and then I think "V, that is a dumbass idea".

— Vyper

**Lost Whisper** is an oubliette (dungeon prison) for "the most dangerous occultists".

## Summary

[edit | edit source]

Seven escaped from Lost Whisper following a failed attempt to execute him via obliteration of the soul.[1] Rumors have spread about his escape.[2]

Lady Geist doesn't want to spend the rest of her life "in an OSIC oubliette".[3]
