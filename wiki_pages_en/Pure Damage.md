# Pure Damage

Pure Damage from Venator's Ira Domini (grey bar only)

**Pure Damage** is a damage type from Abilities and Items that isn't considered Spirit or Weapon Damage, and thus cannot be mitigated by Damage Resistance or Barriers.

## Contents

- 1 Overview
- 2 List of Pure Damage Abilities
- 3 List of Pure Damage Items
- 4 Notes

## Overview

[edit | edit source]

Pure Damage appears as white numbers, and a grey bar in the damage report tab.

Some items can function similarly to Pure Damage by bypassing mitigation of their damage type.

Some abilities or items show as Pure Damage (white numbers), however they are a post damage mitigation damage amp, therefore the damage is already reduced once.

Only effects that make the player Invincible can block Pure Damage. Backdoor Protection can also make Structures block Pure Damage.

## Notes

[edit | edit source]

- Pure Damage does not trigger  Mystic Reverb. If an ability has bonus Pure Damage like the blessed stakes of  Venator's  Ira Domini, it will not be used to calculate the damage of the reverb.
- Pure Damage was added in the Six New Heroes update. Before this, the abilities and items listed dealt Spirit Damage instead.

- Their behavior was similar, bypassing resistances, however some interactions were different, for example Lady Geist's  Soul Exchange worked with  Mystic Reverb.
