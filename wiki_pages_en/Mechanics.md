**Mechanics** in *Deadlock* are the details, particulars, and mechanisms by which a player interacts with the game. This includes the objectives, controls, timing, map, and priorities to consider while playing in order to achieve victory.

Mechanics are generally broken into three categories:

Unit mechanics
The statistics, controls, and statuses of the units of *Deadlock*, including the player's Hero, the marching Troopers, and lane Structures.
Gameplay mechanics
The mechanics of interacting with the game, how the player gains  **** Souls, victory/defeat conditions, damage, healing, and death.
Map mechanics
The locations and entities of the game map and how they interact with players.

## Overview

[edit | edit source]

The overall objective of *Deadlock* is to destroy the enemy team's Patron. This is done over the course of the game, where two teams battle across three lanes, navigating the map using movement tools such as fans, ropes, and Ziplines. Each lane contains a Guardians and a Walkers that a team will need to destroy to progress to and destroy the Patron.

Teams battle AI controlled Troopers, that release Souls when killed. Souls are used to Level-up and buy items.

### Offense and Defense

[edit | edit source]

Weapon Attacks

- Every Hero has a weapon that fires a ranged projectile that allows them to engage in ranged combat to deal damage to other Heroes and units.

Melee Attacks
Main article: Melee Attack

- Every Hero may also perform a melee attack (Default key: Q) to strike at point-blank range. Players can perform either a light or heavy melee attack. Light melee attacks are fast but deal low damage, heavy melee attacks take longer to charge, but have greater damage and range.

Parry

- Parries (Default key: F) are a defensive move that is available to every Hero, in which they can block a melee hit from an enemy. On a successful parry, the enemy is stunned and takes 20% more damage from all sources for 2.75s. The parry goes on cooldown once it's used, but is instantly refunded on a successful parry. Troopers and Guardians can also be parried.

Crouch

- Crouching (Default key: Ctrl) can help avoid enemy fire by making the player smaller and able to hide behind objects.

Hero Abilities

- Every Hero has 4 unique abilities, which they can unlock for use by spending **ability unlocks**. Abilities provide tools for Heroes to enhance their playstyle in several different ways, such as dealing damage, empowering themselves or allied Heroes, weakening enemies, or assisting in traversal.

### Movement

[edit | edit source]

Main article: Movement
Double jump

- Heroes can perform a *double jump* by jumping (Default key: Space) while in mid air, which cost 1 Stamina. By default, Heroes can perform a single *double jump* in the air.

Dash and Air Dash

- Pressing the dash button (Default key: Shift) performs a quick evasive maneuver, which costs 1 Stamina. It can be used to evade enemy attacks, escape fights, or chase enemies.
- Pressing dash while in the air performs an *air dash* which works exactly like a regular dash, except it has less slowdown at the end of the dash. Without items, Heroes can perform a single *air dash* while in the air.
- Most Heroes have unique dash speeds, this can be viewed in their Vitality stats.

Dash Jump

- A *Dash jump* is a fast long jump, which can be performed during a dash and costs 1 Stamina, for a total of 2 including the Stamina spent *dashing*.
- The timing of a *dash jump* is visualized by the Stamina bar turning blue while *dashing.* While the Stamina bar is blue, the player can press jump to successfully *dash jump*.
- If players miss the timing of a *dash jump* by pressing Space too early or too late, the Hero will continue to dash and players will not be able to attempt the *dash jump* again until the initial dash is completed.

Slide

- Heroes can slide by holding crouch while moving faster than 9.6m/s (such as with high move/sprint speed, after dashes, etc) or while going down slopes, preserving momentum and giving infinite ammo for the duration of the slide. This can improve overall DPS by increasing the time before needing to reload when fighting around stairs or slopes, such as the ones seen in every lane next to the Guardian.

Down Dash

- Heroes can quickly dash downwards while in mid air by pressing the crouch button twice, spending 0.5 Stamina.

Wall Jump

- Heroes can perform a *wall jump* by jumping in mid air next to a wall while inputting the direction away from the wall. The first wall jump costs no Stamina, while subsequent wall jumps cost 0.5 Stamina.

Wall Slide

- Heroes can slide along walls while in the air, slowing their descent and limiting their max falling speed to -600 u/s.

Mantle

- Heroes can *mantle*, i.e. climb up obstacles. To mantle, the player must hold the forward key and look in the direction they wish to mantle in while jumping.

Mantle Glide

- Heroes can perform a *mantle glide* by holding the crouch button during a mantle. This will add a speed boost upon the mantle finishing.

### General Strategy

[edit | edit source]

Main article: Strategy
Laning

- Laning is the process of fighting in the lane, collecting Souls from Troopers, and pushing the lane to the enemy Patron.
- There are 3 lanes: York (Yellow), Broadway (Blue), and Park (Green; also referred to as Greenwich). Any part of a lane controlled by the opposing team will be red on the Minimap.
- The **laning phase** refers to the early game where players organize in lanes to gain strength (see: "Strategy").

Ganking

- Ganking is the act of moving from one lane to another to help kill vulnerable enemy Heroes.

Farming

- Farming is the process of focusing on gaining souls and buffs to increase your Hero's strength and buy more items. Farming typically consists of killing Denizen camps and destroying breakables such as Crates, Golden Statues, and Sinner's Sacrifice machines.

### Comeback Mechanics

[edit | edit source]

Main article: Souls

When a team is behind on Souls, there are some mechanics that come into play to allow the losing team to catch up.

- Killing an enemy hero will award more souls if that hero is stronger than the killer team's average networth, regardless of which team is leading in souls.
- The losing team gains up to 30% more souls from Troopers, Denizens, Sinner's Sacrifices and Objectives, based on how behind they are (peaks at 20% max net worth difference). The first 3k in net worth difference is ignored.

- The Soul Urn drop-off location is located in the side lanes if teams are even, but located on the losing team's side if they have significantly less total souls (10% or more difference).
- The Soul Urn will give 130% additional souls to a team that is behind.

## Map Mechanics

[edit | edit source]

Main article: The Cursed Apple

The map is where all of the fighting in *Deadlock* takes place. Known as "The Cursed Apple" and based on Midtown and Lower Manhattan in New York City, the map contains all of the unique features of *Deadlock's* gameplay.

### Minimap

[edit | edit source]

Main article: Minimap

The Minimap provides players with a view of the battle at large, providing live information regarding relevant match information.

- The Minimap provides specific information of the location of Camps, friendly and enemy Troopers, Shops, Buffs, and enemy Heroes (under certain conditions).
- The Minimap should be checked often to give players insight on the status of their own team, lane status, enemy positions, and camp locations.

### Temporary Buffs

[edit | edit source]

Main article: Powerups

Temporary buffs spawn on the bridges overlooking the Soul Urn locations and begin at the 5 minute mark and then every 5 minutes thereafter. Each buff lasts for 160 seconds after being destroyed with a Heavy Melee Attack. The strength of each buff progressively increases from the 5 minute mark to the 40 minute mark.

There are four types of buffs:

1. **Casting Buff:** Grants temporary cooldown reduction and spirit power.

- Starts at +15 Spirit Power and +12% Cooldown Reduction
- Ends at +40 Spirit Power and +25% Cooldown Reduction
- Duration: 160s
1. **Gun Buff:** Grants temporary magazine size and fire rate.

- Starts at +12% Fire Rate and +20% Ammo
- Ends at +35% Fire Rate and +60% Ammo
- Duration: 160s
1. **Movement Buff:** Grants temporary extra stamina and sprint speed.

- Starts at +1 Stamina, +1.5m/s Sprint Speed, +20% Stamina Regeneration Rate, and +40% Zipline Boost
- Ends at +4 Stamina, +4m/s Sprint Speed, +50% Stamina Regeneration Rate, and +80% Zipline Boost
- Duration: 160s
1. **Survival Buff:** Grants temporary health and health regen.

- Starts at +200 Bonus Health and +0.5% Max Health Regen
- Ends at +700 Bonus Health and +1.5% Max Health Regen
- Duration: 160s
