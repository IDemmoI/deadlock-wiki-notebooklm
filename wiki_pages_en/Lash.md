When I come for you, you're gonna know it. There's just not a damn thing that you can do about it.

**Lash** is a Hero in *Deadlock*. He is a brutal bruiser of a Hero, eagerly initiating fights and chasing down fleeing opponents. He leaps into combat with his Grappling hook, Flogging his foes with a life-stealing whip and stomping the ground with a Ground Strike that damages multiple targets. If the enemy team groups up, Lash can pick them all up at once and Death Slam them into the ground.

A champion of an underground fighting arena, Lash has built a reputation as a tough and unsavoury character.

## Trivia

[edit | edit source]

- Jacob Lash is an asshole.
- Lash’s parry animation was changed after streamer JoeyCheerio shared feedback during a livestream that was being watched by Valve Senior Animator Jamaal Bradley. He suggested the move should feel more like a heavy, belly-first chest smash, similar to King K. Rool in Super Smash Bros Ultimate. The updated animation was introduced in a later patch, with Bradley stating "we appreciate our players".[3]
- Lash's name was localized in the Brazilian Portuguese localization as **"Chico T.",** a pun on the word "chicote" ("whip").
- The description for *Deadlock* in Steam Replay 2024 is *"Jacob Lash is an asshole."*
- It appears that at some point in development, Lash really did fight with uppercuts, as an ability named **Shoryuken** was datamined.
- "Thinking About Lash in the Hideout" is displayed as one's status in the in-game Friends List when playing as Lash in the Hideout.
- Lash's killstreak voiceline: "Winning's my business and business is good." is a reference to Megadeth song: "Killing Is My Business... And Business Is Good!".
