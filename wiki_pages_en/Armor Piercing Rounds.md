**Armor Piercing Rounds** is a Tier 4  Weapon Item that can be purchased from the Shop for  **6,400**. Its component,  High-Velocity Rounds, makes up  **800** of the cost.

## Notes

[edit | edit source]

- Armor Piercing Rounds does *not* damage enemies in Invincible state.
- Armor Piercing Rounds bypasses bullet evasion.

## Notable Interactions

[edit | edit source]

- Armor Piercing Rounds can allow the player's bullets to go through  Kelvin's  Frozen Shelter and  McGinnis'  Spectral Wall.
- Armor Piercing Rounds does not bypass  Vyper's  Petrifying Bola.
