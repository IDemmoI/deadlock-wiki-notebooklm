The Venators of St. Benedict are a Vatican-sanctioned order whose members are deployed to regions with a high concentration of supernatural predators.   Few in number, their modus operandi is to observe, identify, and exterminate. They are methodical, they are precise, and once they identify a threat to the flock they are relentless.

But even amongst those who are called The Sword Of St. Benedict, one stands apart from the others.  A man whose hardened heart has allowed him to pay terrible prices in the name of the greater good... and he has once again been dispatched to New York.

## Overview

[edit | edit source]

Venator's mission and motivation in the ritual is to kill the Patrons.[1][2] He's willing to die to achieve his mission.[3]

### Biography

[edit | edit source]

Venator's real name is Quinn Rourke.[4][5] He is commonly referred to by his title, "Father",[5][6] which is ordinary for Catholic priests.

There are less than 24 active Venators,[7] though more have been killed in the past.[8] The Roman numeral "48" under Venator's typography may indicate that he is the 48th Venator. While there are multiple Venators, they usually work alone.[9]

Venator's accent is Irish.

### Personality

[edit | edit source]

Venator showcases that he's willing to do whatever he can for the greater good, burdening himself with the sins he commits for the sake of others.[10][11] His zealous behavior often causes him to be called "angry" and "unhinged".[12] He does, however, judge people based on their actions rather than their appearances or species.[13]

Venator refers to himself as a Carnifex, a Latin word for executioner.[14]

### Information

[edit | edit source]

The Venators of St. Benedict have a friendly rivalry with the Baxter Society.[15] Venator respects the Baxter Society, though he doesn't always agree with them.[16]

## Character Relationships

[edit | edit source]

### Grey Talon

[edit | edit source]

Venator respects Grey Talon, referring to him by his first name and promising to pray for his deceased family.[17][18]

While on opposing teams, Grey Talon wishes they were fighting on the same side.[19]

### Calico

[edit | edit source]

Venator knew Calico from when she was in the Baxter Society, but loathes her now that she's an assassin.[20]

Venator believes that, for the right price, Calico would be willing to kill Ava.[21]

When killing Calico, Venator mentions that it was "for Grey Talon".[22] He also references the 30 pieces of silver that Judas received for betraying Jesus when killing Calico, presumably referring to Calico leaving the Baxter Society.[23]

### Agent Marlowe

[edit | edit source]

Venator is a friend of Marlowe and calls him a "bastard".[24]

### Holliday

[edit | edit source]

Venator prays with Holliday before the ritual to try and help heal her trauma from the Macomb massacre. While it doesn't work, Holliday appreciates Venator for trying.[25]

Venator also warns Holliday to tend her pain and anger before it consumes her.[26]

## Character Interactions

[edit | edit source]

- As a hunter of the wicked, Venator showcases righteous anger towards malevolent creatures and objects, including Abrams' Tome,[27] Graves' lich hand,[28] and Oathkeeper.[29]
- Akin to Silver, Venator doesn't believe in Shiv's change of heart, waiting for him to slip up and ruin his "charade".[30][31] He expresses surprise with Grey Talon's choice of replacement as his protégé, trusting that Shiv should be dead and warning Grey Talon that, if Shiv strays from the path of redemption, the Venators of St. Benedict will come for him.[32]
- Venator prays with Bebop for the sake of Ms. Shelly,[33] and prays with Holliday to try and make her feel better about The Troubadour's massacre in Macomb.[25]
- Venator believes Paige's brother, Bryce, is beyond saving, expressing remorse that she's "thrown away her life to save someone who doesn't deserve it."[34][35]
- Venator and Silver get along with each other, planning to go drink whiskey together after the ritual.[36][37]
- The Archmother and The Hidden King know Venator sees them as his enemy, but each try to convince Venator to work with them to kill the other Patron.[38][39]
- Drifter has killed other Venators before,[40] though he had higher expectations for the playable Venator.[41] If they're on the same team, Venator promises to kill Drifter after the ritual.[42]
- Paradox was expecting Venator to come after her at some point, implying the Paradox Organisation has robbed the Vatican before.[43]
- Vyper likes Venator's voice.[44]
