“

Time to reshape the battlefield.

**McGinnis** is a Hero in *Deadlock.* She wields a machine gun and her abilities revolve around the usage of structures like Mini Turrets and Spectral Walls, which she can create to support allies and hold down positions to the benefit of her team. If the situation goes awry and Medicinal Specter is insufficient, she can unleash a devastating Heavy Barrage at her enemies.

A talented engineer who started her career from a young age, McGinnis isn't fond of theoretical work, thus spending most of her time working in a mechanics shop, often meddling with the supernatural. While the ethics of brainstorming the occult war machine are grey at best, McGinnis isn't that fussed so long as she gets her paycheck.

### Notes

[edit | edit source]

- Services Rendered takes 2 seconds to spin up, increasing McGinnis' fire rate and bullet spread, and slowly winds down at 25% per second (4 seconds total at max spin) as indicated by the grey indicator around the crosshair.
- The DPS and Bullets per second stats listed in game use the max spin up values.
- Services Rendered's base fire rate starts at 5 bullets per second (Cycle time: 0.2) and spins up to 12.5 bullets per second (Cycle time: 0.08), averaging around 11 Bullets per second per clip at the beginning of a match.

Notes

- Turrets have 80% damage resist that decays over 6s, and 35% melee resist that does not decay.
- Turrets deal 50% damage to Troopers and Denizens and 30% damage to Objectives.
- Turret targeting works as follows:

- It will initially target the nearest enemy hero in line of sight when it is placed. If no hero is in sight, it will target the nearest enemy unit.
- It will only switch targets once its initial target dies or leaves sight, or when McGinnis starts shooting a hero in sight, in which case all turrets will attempt to aim at the hero being shot.
- Shooting a unit or hitting an enemy hero with melee attacks, abilities or items will not change the turret's target.
- Turrets are not affected by weapon damage falloff/ramp up, thus normal falloff and the damage bonuses from  Close Quarters,  Point Blank and their upgrades do not apply.
- Turrets yield  **40** souls +  **4** per minute when killed. Part of the bounty can be denied as a Soul Orb.
- Turrets do not receive additional damage from  Monster Rounds and  Cultist Sacrifice.

## Backstory

[edit | edit source]

*See **McGinnis/Lore** for more story content.*

“

Maggie McGinnis always had a knack for building things.  At 4 she was using Lincoln Logs to recreate architectural marvels.  At 10 she built and rewired a custom light fixture for her parent's anniversary.  At 15 she designed a machine that refined the souls of the dead...that last one got a little more attention than the Lincoln Logs.

Now the head of Fairfax Industries Military R&D department, McGinnis spends her time in the machine shop, not in a lab.  She's not interested in theory, to her science is magic you can touch.

## Trivia

[edit | edit source]

- McGinnis' design is similar to popular depictions of the American World War II cultural icon Rosie the Riveter - notably the red headband, blue shirt, jeans overalls, red socks and bulky physique.
- McGinnis and Bebop are currently tied for the highest base ammo count at 66.
- In *The Binding of Oathkeeper* artwork, McGinnis has a mermaid tattoo on her left shoulder.
- McGinnis' Heavy Barrage sound effects were changed to sound less like a real air raid siren.
- "Tinkering in the Hideout" is displayed as one's status in the in-game Friends List when playing as McGinnis in the Hideout.
