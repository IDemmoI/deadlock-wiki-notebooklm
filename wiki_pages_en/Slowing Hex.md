**Slowing Hex** is a Tier 2  Spirit Item that can be purchased from the Shop for  **1,600**.

It is a component of  Vortex Web.

## Notes

[edit | edit source]

- Slowing Hex sets a player's gravity to 120%, regardless of their base gravity. This especially affects  Celeste and  Ivy, who have innate gravity reduction.
- Slowing Hex does not disable passive effects such as  Vyper's  Slither.
- The Doorman's  Doorway and  Mo & Krill's  Burrow are *not* affected by Slowing Hex.
