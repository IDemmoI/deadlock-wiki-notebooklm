**Monster Rounds** is a Tier 1  Weapon Item that can be purchased from the Shop for  **800**.

It is a component of  Cultist Sacrifice.

## Notes

[edit | edit source]

- Monster Rounds applies additional damage to Non-Player Characters. This includes Troopers, lane structures like Guardians, Walkers, neutral entities such as Denizen, and the Patron.
- It does apply additional damage against  Graves'  Ghouls, but *not* against  McGinnis'  Mini Turrets.
