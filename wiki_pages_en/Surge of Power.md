**Surge of Power** is a Tier 3  Spirit Item that can be purchased from the Shop for  **3,200**. Its component,  Extra Spirit, makes up  **800** of the cost.

## Notes

[edit | edit source]

- Despite the in-game tooltip specifically referring to Move Speed, the duration also applies to the Fire Rate bonus.
