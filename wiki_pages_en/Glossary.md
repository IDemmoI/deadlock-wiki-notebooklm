# Glossary

“Alright buddy, give 'em hell!”

This page is under construction. To avoid edit conflicts, please consult the edit history and the talk page.

This page has a glossary of gameplay terms commonly used by the Deadlock community. Most of these terms have been adopted from other MOBA games.

## A-Z

[edit | edit source]

**BKB**

- Dota's equivalent of Unstoppable

**Carry**

- A type of hero who tends to start out weak but scales considerably with Souls

**Creep**

- Dota's equivalent of Trooper. Rarely used to refer to Denizen

**Debuff Remover**

- Old name for Dispel Magic

**Frontliner**

- A type of hero who is relatively durable and seeks to disrupt enemy players

**Fountain**

- Spawn room

**Hook**

- Bebop's Grapple Arm. Rarely used to refer to Yamato's Flying Slash

**M1**

- Gun

**Remlings**

- Rem's Lil Helpers

**Tower**

- Dota's equivalent of Guardian
