As a Scientist, Professor Dynamo always had an inquisitive mind.  So when a rift in space and time opened up in central park he was first on the scene to investigate – and while he expected such a majestic sight to be life changing, he was thinking more in a metaphorical way as opposed to having his body atomized and reduced to a tiny dying star.

But while his body is gone, his spirit and lust for knowledge lives on.  A tenured professor at Columbia College, Dynamo looks to learn more about his condition while maintaining his class load, after all: he has future minds to inspire.

## Overview

[edit | edit source]

Dynamo's wish and motivation in the ritual is to get his human body back.[1][2][3]

### Biography

[edit | edit source]

Dynamo's youth as an undergraduate student involved a lot of fist fights.[4][5][6]

Dynamo lost his body 50 years before the ritual, during the first Maelstrom.[7][8]

Dynamo has not touched an Astral Gate since his body was transformed,[9] although he has done a lot of research into Astral Gates.[10][11]

### Personality

[edit | edit source]

Dynamo generally prefers working with people as opposed to lying to them, which is why he doesn't enjoy games like Poker.[12]

In a Winter season voice line, Dynamo says he didn't sleep the night before the Maelstrom because he was "afraid of death".[13]

Most characters are apologetic when fighting Dynamo.[14]

### Information

[edit | edit source]

Dynamo built and designed his own gun, the "Vonnegun".[15]

Dynamo's connections in science and academia are described as "vast".[16]

Dynamo likes to go to the New York Library to think.[17] He hopes to visit South Ixia someday.[18] Dynamo isn't a fan of the Occult Industrial Complex.[19] Dynamo appreciates the work of the Baxter Society.[20]

## Character Relationships

[edit | edit source]

### Marla

[edit | edit source]

Marla is Dynamo's wife.[21] They have been married for 31 years.[22]

Marla bakes Dynamo key lime pies, even though he can't eat them.[23][24]

The first thing Dynamo intends to do once he gets his body back is to "give Marla a hug and not let go for about 3 months".[25]

Dynamo and Marla have visited the Paradox Museum together.[26]

During the winter season, Dynamo and Marla go carolling together.[27]

### Pocket and Maximilian Fairfax

[edit | edit source]

Dynamo is Pocket's godfather. He recognizes Pocket and says he would have helped them if he knew they were alive. Pocket didn't tell Dynamo that they survived because they weren't sure if they could trust him.[28][29]

Dynamo used to be friends with Maximilian Fairfax. Dynamo believes Fairfax has "warm nostalgia" for him and considers Fairfax a "complicated man" as opposed to a bad man.[30]

### Sophia Arroyo

[edit | edit source]

Ivy's sister, Sophia Arroyo, is studying at Columbia under Dynamo.[31]

Sophia speaks highly of Dynamo,[32][33] and Dynamo thinks she's a great student.[34]

Ivy worries her fighting Dynamo during the ritual would preconceive him against Sophia.[35] Dynamo reaffirms Ivy that Sophia's grades won't be affected by Ivy's actions in the ritual.[36]

### Kelvin

[edit | edit source]

Dynamo and Kelvin are friends. They last saw each other at the Black Château, before Kelvin disappeared.[37]

Dynamo is a fan of Kelvin's books.[38]

Dynamo sometimes refers to Kelvin as "Kevin".[39]

## Character Interactions

[edit | edit source]

- Dynamo's lust for knowledge leads him to ask questions about other characters, such as how  Seven survived his execution,[40] how  Paradox's time manipulation works,[41] what the Djinn are like,[42] what the contents of  Abrams' Tome and  Pocket's case could be,[43][44] what  Viscous is,[45] and if  Yamato's sword is an heirloom.[46]
- Dynamo showcases courage by telling  Abrams,[47]  Shiv[48] and  Warden[49] that he'll stay close to them during the fight.
- Dynamo defends his wife's honor from  Lash's insults.[50]
- Dynamo warns  Wraith not to take advantage of his students who frequent her casino, otherwise he'll step in.[51]
- Dynamo asks  Infernus to help him pick a hat at a haberdashery.[52]
- Haze tells Dynamo that OSIC agent Marlowe has an offer for Dynamo to join them. Dynamo declines, claiming to be happy in the "Private sector". Dynamo mistakes Marlowe for his wife, Marla.[53]
- McGinnis offers to upgrade Dynamo's body, although Dynamo declines as he intends to get his human body back after the ritual.[54]
- While on the same team,  Drifter threatens to kill Marla if Dynamo fails the ritual.[55]
- Grey Talon respects Dynamo's courage for enduring his body being torn apart and wishes he had the same optimism Dynamo has.[56]
- Vindicta wants to speak to Dynamo if they fail the ritual, due to John Hathorne planning to give a speech at Columbia College.[57]
- Mirage asks Dynamo if he can give him and Nashala a tour of Columbia College.[58]
- Seven is a fan of Dynamo's research into Astral Gates and holds him in high regard.[59][60]
- Lady Geist and  Mina dislike Dynamo's jacket.[61][62]  Graves thinks the jacket is cool.[63]
- Rem says Dynamo smells like banana bread.[64]

## Removed Lore

[edit | edit source]

Dynamo originally had unused monologue lines that seemed be planned for something unrelated to the ritual. In these lines, Dynamo references himself and Maximilian Fairfax being in a group called "the squid boys". These lines have since been removed, but are available on Dynamo's quotes page.[68]

Dynamo used to have a line about playing at  Wraith's weekly poker games.[69]

### Neon Prime - Sumo

[edit | edit source]

Dynamo was named **Sumo** in *Neon Prime*, and later renamed to **Jeong**. He was renamed to Dynamo still during *Neon Prime*.

There was also an unused concept for Dynamo depicting him as a pizza delivery driver.
