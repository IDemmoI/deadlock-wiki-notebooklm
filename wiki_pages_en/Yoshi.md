# Yoshi

Yoshi's profile picture

**Yoshi** is a *Deadlock* developer.[1] Their username in the official *Deadlock* Discord is *@yoshideadlock*.

## Overview

[edit | edit source]

Yoshi is known for being the developer at Valve who most often engages with the *Deadlock* community through the official Discord and forums, as well as posting announcements and patch notes. They are nicknamed after the character Yoshi from *Super Mario Bros.*

Yoshi's real identity is currently unknown, but it has been confirmed to be an individual account.[2]
