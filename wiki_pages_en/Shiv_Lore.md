A loyal member of "The Baxter Society", an international order of monster hunters, Shiv travels across North America looking to put down creatures that prey on the living.

While his less polished nature and criminal past make some of the more traditional members of the Society wary, no one can deny his effectiveness.

## Overview

[edit | edit source]

Shiv is fighting in the ritual on behalf of the Baxter Society.[1][2] His wish is unknown, but Shiv's motivation is to prove his worth to  Grey Talon and the Baxter Society.[3][4][5]

### Biography

[edit | edit source]

Not much is known about Shiv's criminal past from before he joined the Baxter Society. The Hidden King calls Shiv's past "bloody",[6] Paige calls Shiv a "man with a tortured past",[7] and the Venators of St. Benedict are keeping an eye on Shiv in case he strays from the path of redemption.[8] Shiv's claims that the Baxter Society "saved his life",[9] and his change of heart seems to be genuine.[10]Shiv was chosen by Grey Talon as his replacement in the Baxter Society.[11][12][13] He has been in the Society for "a few years" and says he is not respected by the other members.[14] Shiv worries that the Baxter Society would eventually turn on him,[15][16] as they still doubt that he's changed from his past.[17] It's implied that Shiv feels lonely.[18]

Shiv allegedly "took something that doesn't belong to him".[19]

### Personality

[edit | edit source]

Shiv likes to go bowling and drink bourbon.[20][21] He is also referred to as a biker.[22]

Shiv hates cats.[23]

## Character Relationships

[edit | edit source]

### Grey Talon

[edit | edit source]

Grey Talon has high hopes for Shiv, despite his criminal past, and holds him in high regard, placing Shiv in the top 8 of people he'd want fighting alongside him.[17][24] While the conversations are unfinished, Grey Talon seems to defend Shiv's change of heart from Silver and Venator's doubts.[25][8]

### Drifter

[edit | edit source]

Drifter knew Shiv before he joined the Baxter Society.[26] If he kills Shiv, he calls him a "disappointment" and mentions that killing him feels "Shakespearean".[27][28][29] He also mentions that, had Shiv not "put himself in a muzzle, maybe he'd be worried".[30] When hunting Shiv, he refers to himself as an "old friend".[31] How Shiv and Drifter met each other is unknown.

### Silver

[edit | edit source]

Silver loathes Shiv with a passion and refuses to believe he could change from his criminal past.[32][33] She believes he deserves a fate worse than death.[34] How Silver and Shiv met each other and why Silver hates Shiv is currently unknown.

## Character Interactions

[edit | edit source]

- Multiple characters compliment Shiv's appearance, calling him "pretty boy",[35] "handsome bastard",[36] "impossibly handsome asshole",[37] and a "shampoo ad with knives".[38]
- Shiv respects Holliday and offers her a place at the Baxter Society.[39] If they're on opposing teams, Shiv promises to kill the Troubadour on Holliday's behalf.[40] Shiv loves Holliday's hat.[41]
- Shiv respects Lash's moxie.[42]
- Venator doesn't believe in Shiv's change of heart, waiting for him to slip up and ruin his "charade".[43][44]
- Lady Geist calls Shiv attractive and asks to have a drink with him after the ritual.[45]
- Ivy expresses admiration for Shiv.[46]  McGinnis wants to talk to Shiv and "fix him".[47][48]  Yamato will simply remark "nice hair".[49]
- Haze and Vyper respect Shiv's taste in knives.[50][51]
- Celeste "doesn't get the appeal" of Shiv and says he has "ruined a lot of lives".[52][53]
- Paige thinks Shiv pulls off the "bad boy thing" better than Lash does.[54] Paige likes Shiv, although she prefers Mirage.[55]

## Removed Lore

[edit | edit source]

In removed voice lines, the Patrons said that Shiv "had been" a father[56] and had a "monster inside him" called "The Beast".[57] These lines were removed in the Old Gods, New Blood update.
