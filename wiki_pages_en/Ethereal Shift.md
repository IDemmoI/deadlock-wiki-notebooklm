**Ethereal Shift** is a Tier 4  Spirit Item that can be purchased from the Shop for  **6,400**.

## Contents

- 1 Notes
- 2 Update history
- 3 Trivia
- 4 Navigation

## Notes

[edit | edit source]

- During Ethereal Shift, caster can only float around slowly at 3 m/s. Caster does not fall if mid-air. The caster can still turn their camera and character.
- Casting Ethereal Shift does not reset jump/dash, even if mid-air when cast.
- Casting Ethereal Shift cancels any abilities currently being channeled.
