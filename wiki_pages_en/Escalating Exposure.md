**Escalating Exposure** is a Tier 4  Spirit Item that can be purchased from the Shop for  **6,400**. Its component,  Mystic Vulnerability, makes up  **1,600** of the cost.

## Notes

[edit | edit source]

- Unlike its component  Mystic Vulnerability, the amplified damage from Escalating Exposure is a separate instance of damage, applied after the original spirit damage. This has implications for abilities or effects that rely on single instances of damage, such as  Tankbuster.

- This still applies even if the teammate also has Escalating Exposure.
- The user that most recently started applying stacks' icon will be the only one shown to the entire team.
- The user with stacks applied to them will only see the combined total of Escalating Exposure stacks on them, not each player's individual count.
