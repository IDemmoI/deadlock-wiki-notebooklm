## Menu

[edit | edit source]

### Select

[edit | edit source]

Select lines are played when selecting a Hero to search for a match, or increasing a Hero's matchmaking priority.

### Unselect

[edit | edit source]

Unselect lines are played when unselecting a Hero before searching for a match, or reducing a Hero's matchmaking priority.

## Conversations

[edit | edit source]

Conversations are played in the zipline at the start of the match. If a conversation is incomplete, only the first line is played. If the Hero has no conversations with their lane partner, a Patron Match Start line is played instead.

## Ability Lines

[edit | edit source]

### Upgrade

[edit | edit source]

Upgrade lines are played when unlocking and upgrading abilities.

### Use

[edit | edit source]

Usage lines are played when using an ability.

### Use (Low Health)

[edit | edit source]

Variation of Usage lines, played when the Hero has less than 20% health. Labeled as "desperation".

## Pings

[edit | edit source]

**Pings** or **voice commands** are lines played when the player marks a location, another player, or wants to send a message to their team. Pings also appear as text messages.

Pings are triggered by pressing Mouse 3, holding Mouse 3 to open the Chat Wheel menu, or holding Tab and clicking on the map with Mouse 1 or clicking on another player's portrait or items with Mouse 3.

The Chat Wheel menu can be customized in settings.

### Context Pings

[edit | edit source]

Context Pings are a unique option in the Chat Wheel that shows a ping based on the player's current state in the game, or as a response to another player's ping or actions. Various pings can appear as context pings.

### Headed to Lane/Base

[edit | edit source]

The player can ping where they are going by aiming at the location and clicking Mouse 3, selecting the option in the Chat Wheel, or holding Tab and clicking in the map close to the location they want to go. It appears as a context ping when riding a zipline to the lane or to the base. Using line of sight or clicking on the map shows a green arrow at the marked location.

### See Enemy

[edit | edit source]

An enemy's location can be pinged by aiming at the enemy and clicking Mouse 3, or clicking at the enemy's icon in the Minimap. This will also highlight the enemy in red for the player and allies.

### Missing Enemy

[edit | edit source]

A missing enemy can be pinged by holding Tab and clicking Mouse 3 at the enemy's portrait.

### Warn Ally

[edit | edit source]

An ally can be warned of danger by aiming at them and clicking Mouse 3, or holding Tab and clicking at their portrait or Minimap icon with Mouse 3.

## Patrons

[edit | edit source]

### Match Start

[edit | edit source]

Patrons' Match Start lines are played in the zipline at the beginning of the match. They are hero-specific.

### Big Healing

[edit | edit source]

Big Healing lines are played when a Hero has healed its allies for a total of 5000 HP.

## Unused Lines

[edit | edit source]

### Win and Lose

[edit | edit source]

Win and Lose are lines in which the Heroes react to either winning or losing the match. There are specific lines for winning or losing short games (less than 30 minutes) and long games (more than 50 minutes).

### Pass on zipline

[edit | edit source]

"Pass on zipline" lines are intended to be played when two ally Heroes cross paths on a zipline. Based on the context, the Hero who says it is going back to the base.

### Killed By

[edit | edit source]

"Killed By" lines were responses to being killed by specific enemy Heroes. These lines were only present in *Neon Prime*.
