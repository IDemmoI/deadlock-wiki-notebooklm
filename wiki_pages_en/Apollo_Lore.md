A land of beauty, tradition and conflict; North Ixia is no stranger to brutal political violence. So when Apollo was told that it was for his own safety he was being sent to New York for school, he knew the situation was serious.

Adjusting to life at Blackmore Academy didn’t come easy to Apollo. His school was supposed to be filled with The Cursed Apple’s best and brightest but all he saw, with few exceptions, was a sea of aggressive mediocrity. Electing not to waste his time with frivolous things like parties and dances, he instead put his energy on the piste, where he rigorously trains as captain of the Blackmore fencing team.

However, at the end of the day Apollo does not fret too much on the tedium of life in New York. He knows that eventually the time will come when his father will call on him to come home and he will get to show that he is worthy of wielding his family's ancestral blade.

## Overview

[edit | edit source]

Apollo's wish is unknown, but he seems to be fighting for the sake of Ixia.[1][2] While mentioned that Apollo's father expects great things from him,[3] Apollo claims that he isn't fighting in the ritual for his father.[4]

### Biography

[edit | edit source]

Apollo is 18 years old[5] and is referred to as "*the Prince of Ixia"*[6] or "*Crown Prince of North Ixia",* though he finds the title inaccurate.[7]

Apollo was taught fencing at a young age, having learned Riposte when he was only 5 years old.[8] He seems to be a fencing champion,[9] and hopes to one day represent Ixia at the Olympics.[10][11] Apollo inherited his sword from his father.[12]

### Personality

[edit | edit source]

Apollo holds his Ixian culture in very high regard.[13][14] He refers to the other playable Ixians,  Abrams and  Infernus, as his "brothers".[15][16] While he doesn't hate humans, he believes Ixians are above them.[17][18] Apollo is content at Blackmore Academy,[19] but he refuses to see New York as his home and is only there because his father wanted him to be.[20][21]

Apollo is extremely confident in his skills and believes himself to be the best.[22][23] He holds honor and discipline in high regards.[24][25][26] His attitude often causes other characters to regard him as vain.[27][28]

### Information

[edit | edit source]

Ixian is Apollo's native language.[29] His ultimate "*Itani Lo Sahn*" is Ixian for "*I have no equal*".[30]

Apollo says his life has been personally affected by political assassinations.[31] Notably, while he speaks often of his father, he never mentions his mother.

The symbol etched in Disengaging Sigil is Apollo's family sigil.[32] He has fond memories of his father teaching him how to cast it.[33]

Apollo has no siblings.[34]

Apollo believes Maximilian Fairfax is a "great man".[35] Both Apollo and his father are fans of Fairfax Industries.[36]

## Character Relationships

[edit | edit source]

### Apollo's father

[edit | edit source]

Apollo's father is referred to as a warlord, and has the authority equivalent to a governor and a general.[37]

Apollo is proud of his father and believes his father is proud of him.[38] His father says "loyalty is everything" and "the first step to victory begins with crushing the enemy's hope".[39][40]

When killing  Silver, Apollo mentions that his father would've turned her into a cloak.[41]

## Character Interactions

[edit | edit source]

- Apollo and  Graves are classmates, and are each other's lab partners.[42]

- Apollo is shocked that  Infernus doesn't speak Ixian nor care for Ixian culture.[43][44]

- Apollo respects  Ivy but believes she shouldn't have saved Spanish Harlem, believing that they should've saved themselves rather than rely solely on Ivy.[45][46]

- Apollo respects  Mirage and  Warden for their conviction and honor.[47][48]

- Apollo sends his father's regards to Ambassador Nashala.[49][50]

- Apollo accidentally invites  Viscous to one of his father's galas.[51]

- Apollo commends  Vindicta for her war against the Friends of Humanity, although he dislikes her dishonorable tactics.[52][53]

- Haze is worried about Apollo participating in the ritual since him dying would cause an international incident.[54][55]

- Krill compares Apollo to a teenage version of  Lash.[56]

- Apollo has stayed at The Baroness hotel,[57] and has tipped  The Doorman.[58]
