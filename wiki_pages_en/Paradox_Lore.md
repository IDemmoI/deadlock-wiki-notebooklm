Paradox.  An artifact of time, and the name of a notorious thieves' guild... one that prides themselves on constructing elaborate heists that target the most untouchable of individuals and institutions.  What does Paradox do with the countless artifacts, state secrets, and celebrity paternity tests that they have stolen?  Put it on display at pop-up museums, so that they can show the world that no one is out of Paradox’s reach.

Shrouded in mystery, each member of Paradox dons a mask and takes on the organization's name.  They are everywhere.  They are nowhere.  And if they want something, they are inevitable.

## Overview

[edit | edit source]

Paradox has a few motivations in the ritual, the main one being to prove herself to the Paradox Organisation, partially for bragging rights.[1][2][3][4] It's implied that one of Paradox's goals in the ritual is to steal something from the opposing Patron (such as the Hidden King's crown) and display it at the Paradox Museum.[5][6][3] It's also implied that Paradox wants to show that she's fit to lead the Paradox Organisation by completing the ritual.[7] Her exact wish is unknown, but there are dozens of possibilities according to the Patrons.[8]

### Biography

[edit | edit source]

Not much is known about Paradox herself. She's Scottish and a "bored rich person",[9][10] who gladly gave up her identity to join the Paradox Organisation.[11] The helmet Paradox wears can be taken off (as it's just a mask),[12] but what Paradox does in her free time is unknown.

Paradox is nicknamed as "The Time Thief".[13]

Paradox is currently trying to find a way to break into the Baxter Society's manor house,[14] the Baroness Hotel,[15] and  McGinnis' lab.[16] Paradox was expecting  Venator to come after her at some point, implying the Paradox Organisation has robbed the Vatican before.[17]

### Personality

[edit | edit source]

Paradox is playful and jokey, but takes the name of the Paradox Organisation very seriously. She drops the humor if Paradox gets threatened or disrespected.[18][19][20]

Other characters often call Paradox "clever".[21]

### Information

[edit | edit source]

How the time manipulation Paradox uses works is a tightly held secret.[22] Paradox says time travel "isn't a thing yet".[23]

## Character Interactions

[edit | edit source]

- Paradox is amazed by the craftsmanship used to create  Bebop.[26]
- Paradox is interested in doing business with  Wraith.[27]
- Paradox knows exactly who  Pocket is.[28][29]
- Paradox loves messing with  Lady Geist and  Kelvin.[30][31]
- Paradox likes  Calico,  Mo & Krill and  Viscous.[32][33][34]
- Paradox loves  Warden's conviction.[35]
- Kelvin is one of Paradox's targets.[36]
- Seven wants to commission Paradox for a job.[37]
- Lady Geist has an old grudge against Paradox.[38]  The Paradox Organisation has stolen one of her husband's paintings.[39][40]
- Holliday,[41]  Kelvin,[42]  Seven[43] and  Warden[44] call out Paradox for wasting their power on petty crimes.
- Drifter,[45] Ivy,[46] Paige,[47] Rem[48] and Vyper[49] like Paradox's trench coat.
- Both  Raven and Paradox claim to be in possession of the Tome of Anubis.  Wraith says she thought the Tome was at the Met.[50][51]

- At some point, the Tome of Anubis was reportedly seen in New York's black market.[52]

### Thefts

[edit | edit source]

Paradox has shown interest in stealing a wide variety of objects belonging to the other characters. Some of these objects include:

- Abrams' Tome.[53]
- Celeste's horn.[54]
- Holliday's revolver.[55]
- McGinnis' research and secrets she holds for Fairfax.[56][57]
- Nashala.[58]
- Paige's rare books.[59]
- Pocket's suitcase.[60]
- Yamato's sword.[61]

There are also some things Paradox has successfully stolen prior to the ritual, such as:

- Seven's safehouse location.[62]
