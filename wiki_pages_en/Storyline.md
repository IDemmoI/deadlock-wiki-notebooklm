## Timeline

[edit | edit source]

The Deadlock timeline and time period are kept purposefully ambiguous, as the game is still in development and story aspects are subject to change. However, based on context clues such as references to the 1949 musical South Pacific[1][2][3][4] by various characters, it can be inferred that the game takes place circa the 1930s-1940s (see *Historical References* below). The buildings in the Cursed Apple use architectural references from New York City up to 1932.[5] Even so, there are visual references from later in the 20th century: Shiv's design is reminiscent of a 1950s Greaser, while Billy is inspired by 1970s Punks. The computer in the Hideout is similar to the 1973 Xerox Alto, one of the first personal computers, albeit with a typewriter keyboard.

The timeline below is based on mentioned in-game events and the Lady Geist visual novel. Events without a specific year use the date of the ritual as a reference point.

## Branching Timelines

[edit | edit source]

Due to the multiple choice aspect of the *Deadlock* visual novels, there are multiple potential timelines of events that happened shortly before and after the ritual.

## Trivia

[edit | edit source]

- Some posters on the map used to show specific dates, such as a Jazz Festival poster dated 1927 and a Rescue Beam poster dated 1939. Those posters have either been edited or removed from the map over time.
- The synthetic fiber Kevlar, which the item  Diviner's Kevlar is named after, was invented in 1965.
