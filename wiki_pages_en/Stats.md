# Stats

Stats are divided into three categories: **Weapon**, **Vitality**, and **Spirit**. Stats can be modified by purchasing items, leveling up, gaining buffs, or through spirit power scaling.

See Hero Comparison Table for a full list and comparison of all hero stats.
