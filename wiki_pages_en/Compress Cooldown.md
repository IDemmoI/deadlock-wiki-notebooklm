**Compress Cooldown** is a Tier 2  Spirit Item that can be purchased from the Shop for  **1,600**.

It is a component of  Superior Cooldown.

- 1 Notes
- 2 Update history

## Notes

[edit | edit source]

- If Compress Cooldown is imbued on  Sinclair's  Audience Participation, it does not reduce the cooldown of copied ultimates, only the default cooldown of the ability.
