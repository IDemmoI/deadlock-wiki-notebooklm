# Falloff Range

**Falloff Range** is a stat that determines the minimum and maximum distance in which a Hero can deal Weapon Damage to a target.

## Contents

- 1 Overview

- 1.1 Explosive bullets
- 2 Falloff Range
- 3 Sources

## Overview

[edit | edit source]

For most heroes, weapon damage falls off by -90% at maximum range and beyond, following a linear scale from minimum range. See "Base Falloff Range" table below for exceptions.

The following heroes have exceptions to falloff range:

- Apollo's weapon has no damage falloff and deals no damage beyond its max range.
- Bebop's weapon has a separate Max Range stat that increases with Boons, and deals no damage beyond its max range.
- Drifter's and  Graves' weapons deal no damage beyond their max range.
- Silver (Transformed)'s weapon has a fixed range that cannot be increased.

Falloff range can be increased with Items.

### Explosive bullets

[edit | edit source]

Alternate fire weapons with explosive bullets deal 50% damage at maximum explosion radius, multiplied by the regular damage falloff.
