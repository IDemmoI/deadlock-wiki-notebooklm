# Vasili Zorin

**Vasili Zorin** is a *Deadlock* developer. He is best known for his work as a concept artist on *Sunset Overdrive* and *Overwatch*.

Vasili was the character designer of *Neon Prime*.[1] It is believed that he still works on *Deadlock* as a character designer and concept artist.

In a January 2024 interview, Vasili said he was working on a then-unannounced project with Icefrog.[2]
