## Overview

[edit | edit source]

Warden's motivation in the ritual seems to be to stop the ritual and prevent the Patrons from being summoned.[1][2][3]

### Biography

[edit | edit source]

Before Warden was born, Warden's parents were living in New York, having neglected their duties in preparing for a mystic apocalypse. When the First Maelstrom occured, making the public aware of the existence of magic, Warden's parents realised that everything they had been told was true, and that the "mystic apocalypse" they had been warned about was now on the horizon. Having "failed to protect" New York,[4][5] Warden's parents made sure that their son, Warden, would not make the same mistake they did.[1] It's implied that Warden's militant alchemist bloodline comes from his father's side.[6]

Warden is said to be a vigilante,[7][8] although his feats are currently unknown. Warden mentions that New York is under his protection.[9]

Warden has no hobbies outside of his training.[10][11][12] Due to training his entire life,[13] Warden never got to live a normal childhood.[14] It's implied that he dreams of playing catch.[15]

How Warden intends to stop the Patrons is currently unknown, although Warden expresses that he "only gets one shot at this",[16] and is willing to die if it means completing his mission.[17]

### Personality

[edit | edit source]

It's implied that Warden is miserable.[18][19]

Warden is implied to have trouble trusting people.[20] He seems to hide his motivations from his allies, based on his conversation with Holliday.[21]

Although Warden is committed to protecting humanity at all costs, he does not discriminate against supernatural creatures, instead treating characters like Abrams and Ivy with respect.[22][23]

### Information

[edit | edit source]

Despite his appearance, Warden is not a police officer. A concept sketch for Warden's upcoming redesign can be seen in one of Yamato's concept artwork released by Yoshi.[24] The Shopkeeper may ask Warden if he would be interested in selling his cape, despite Warden's current model not having one.[25] The Newscaster voice line for Warden's Winter skin challenge also makes a reference to his upcoming redesign, asking "What will he look like next?"[26]

## Character Relationships

[edit | edit source]

### The Patrons

[edit | edit source]

Warden hides his true motives from the Patrons, despite their telepathic abilities.[27][28] As such, the Patrons likely don't know that Warden is working against their goals. He is unable to hide his emotions from them, as the Patrons' heralds identify that Warden hates the Patrons and is afraid of failure.[29][30]

Warden views his battle with the Patrons as a war.[31][32]

### Grey Talon

[edit | edit source]

Warden once looked up to Grey Talon and heavily respects him.[33][34] When on opposing teams, Warden is shocked that Talon would willingly work for the Patrons.[35] While Grey Talon appreciates Warden's conviction,[36] he believes that both he and Warden live "hollow lives."[37]

## Character Interactions

[edit | edit source]

- Warden respects Ivy,[23] but he also believes she's "meddling in things she doesn't understand."[38]
- The Shopkeeper, Viscous and Vyper point out that Warden never smiles.[39][18][40]  Dynamo and  Lady Geist tell Warden that he looks "dour",[41] with Lady Geist also describing him as looking "perpetually miserable".[19]  Venator says that Warden fights as someone who wouldn't have chosen the life he has.[42]

- An unfinished conversation with  Grey Talon implies that, if Warden had a choice, he wouldn't have trained his whole life to fight the Patrons (Note that as Warden's own line is missing, it is uncertain if he accepts or denies it- Grey Talon merely expresses sorrow at his response).[43]
- Holliday thinks Warden has issues and calls him a "creepy homeschooled kid".[44][45] If they're on opposing teams, however, Holliday wishes that they were working together.[46]
- Like the Patrons, The Doorman is also unable to find out exactly what Warden wants, even when Warden is trapped in the Baroness Hotel.[47] He seems familiar with Warden's parents and derides them as failures.[6][48]
- Apollo respects Warden for his conviction and discipline.[49]
- Billy,[50]  Calico,[51]  Pocket[52] and  Mirage[53] call Warden out for his blind devotion to his parents' mission.

## Removed Lore

[edit | edit source]

### Neon Prime - Murphy

[edit | edit source]

Warden was named **Murphy** in *Neon Prime*. This name was reused for the new character of Captain Murphy in Holliday's visual novel. He may have been named after the protagonist of the 1987 movie *Robocop,* as Murphy was a robot peacekeeper in *Neon Prime's* sci-fi setting.
