**Cultist Sacrifice** is a Tier 3  Weapon Item that can be purchased from the Shop for  **3,200**. Its component,  Monster Rounds, makes up  **800** of the cost.

## Notes

[edit | edit source]

- Using Cultist Sacrifice on an NPC gives the player the full soul bounty plus 170% (270% total). The original bounty and the bonus are displayed as two separate values when activated.
- The buff persists through death.
- Cultist Sacrifice applies additional damage to Non-Player Characters. This includes Troopers, lane structures like Guardians, Walkers, neutral entities such as Denizens, and the Patron.
- It does apply additional damage against  Graves'  Ghouls, but *not* against  McGinnis'  Mini Turrets. However, both of them can be consumed for the buff and bonus souls.
- Cultist Sacrifice's active counts as Spirit Damage. This means it can activate on-hit spirit damage procs such as  Spirit Burn.
- When Cultist Sacrifice comes off cooldown, a whispering voice can be heard demanding a sacrifice.
