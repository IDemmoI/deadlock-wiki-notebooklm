**Spirit Shredder Bullets** is a Tier 2  Weapon Item that can be purchased from the Shop for  **1,600**.

It is a component of  Spirit Rend.

## Notes

[edit | edit source]

- The Spirit Lifesteal from the debuff is independent of the diminishing returns from other sources of spirit lifesteal. It will always grant the player a flat +10% spirit lifesteal.
- The debuff can be applied with melee attacks, as well as abilities that are considered melee attacks.
