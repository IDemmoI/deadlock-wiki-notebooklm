## Overview

[edit | edit source]

Seven's motive and wish in the ritual is to achieve "apotheosis",[1] which means the glorification of a subject to divine level,[2] meaning that Seven wants to become a god, or gain godlike powers.[3][4]

### Biography

[edit | edit source]

Seven claims he was imprisoned in Lost Whisper because he "offended small-minded simpletons with fragile egos".[5] Seven implies getting caught and sent to Lost Whisper was part of his plan.[6] How Seven survived the botched execution is unknown, but he was implied to have died in the process, which could mean he's undead.[7]

Seven has done research on the Astral Gates, and he credits  Professor Dynamo as an influence in his work.[8]

Seven mentions that he once loved someone.[9] According to the Archmother, Seven had a "destiny" which his lover tried to steal.[10]

The OSIC wants Seven dead.[11] They seem to know that Seven is in New York, as Seven is hiding from them in New York's subway tunnels.[12]

Seven mentions that he had to "scurry with the rats since he was a child".[13]

### Personality

[edit | edit source]

Seven says he doesn't like murder, rather, he is "indifferent" to it.[14] He does enjoy letting fear sink into his victims, however.[15]

Seven believes he will become powerful enough for the Patrons to bow to him.[16]

### Information

[edit | edit source]

Seven can speak the Ixian language.[17][18]

The New York Oracle reporter Allie Kincaid is writing a story on Seven.[19][20]

## Character Relationships

[edit | edit source]

### Kelvin

[edit | edit source]

Seven has a one-sided hatred towards Kelvin for unknown reasons.[21][22][23]

Kelvin thinks Seven "seems familiar".[24] When being healed by Kelvin, Seven is reminded of "old times".[25]

Both Seven and Kelvin have an interest on researching Astral Gates.[26][27]

Seven believes Kelvin has a bigger ego than  Lash.[28]

A likely possibility is that Seven is Kelvin's old friend Ettrick, who "vanished".  Lady Geist says Ettrick "caused a scene" at one of Kelvin's fundraisers.[29] This is supported by one of Seven's lines to Kelvin that mirrors one of Kelvin's lines about Ettrick.[30][31] Kelvin pushed Ettrick away due to his "ambition", which aligns with Seven's ambition to achieve apotheosis.[32] Seven also believes that mentors "use the young to achieve glories they could not achieve on their own", possibly referring to Kelvin.[33]

## Character Interactions

[edit | edit source]

- Seven has a strong conviction that exceptional magic users should use their powers, regardless of how others might judge them for it. He criticizes  Dynamo for wanting to "throw away" the "great gift" of his star form,[34] teases  Infernus for "putting on a muzzle" and "wasting" his powers,[35][36] criticizes  Celeste for masking her power at a sideshow,[37] and says  McGinnis is "wasting her brilliance" working for Fairfax Industries.[38]

- Seven respects  Ivy for "seeing the world as it was and deciding it was not acceptable to her".[39]
- Seven wants to commission  Paradox for a job.[40]
- Seven knows that  The Doorman isn't normal and wants to uncover his secrets.[41][42]
- Seven warns  Graves that her mentor is using her to "live glories they could not achieve on their own".[35] He also warns Graves that the path she's on will eventually get her killed. Seven warns Graves because he "hates to see talent wasted".[43]

- Graves would like to be Seven's "intern".[44]
- Seven warns  Rem to stay away from anyone who mentions the OSIC, claiming that they won't help Rem get home.[45] Rem finds Seven scary.[46]
- When on the same team, Seven teases  Haze for working alongside him and asks how soon will they start fighting each other once the ritual is over.[47][48]
- McGinnis offers help to remove Seven's headgear.[49]
- Paradox is aware of Seven's safehouse in the tunnels and threatens to reveal their location.[50]
- Paige swears she won't let her brother turn out like Seven.[51]
- The Doorman thinks Seven is more delusional than  Lash.[52]
- According to  Rem, Seven smells like "evil s'mores".[53] This contrasts  Kelvin's scent of "nice s'mores."[54]
