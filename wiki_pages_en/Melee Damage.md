# Melee Damage

Melee Damage is a stat that Heroes start with and determines how much damage a hero's Melee Attack deals. Melee damage scales with Boons and Items. Melee damage scales with the Weapon Damage stat at a rate of 50%.

Most heroes have the same base melee damage and scaling, with a few exceptions (see table below).
