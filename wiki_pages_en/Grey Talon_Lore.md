A founding member of "The Baxter Society", Wesley Grey Talon spent close to 40 years hunting monsters that preyed on man before finally deciding to find a replacement and lay down his bow. Grey Talon's duties had meant that he never spent much time with his wife and kid, and now that his son had started a family of his own he realized that this was his last chance to reconnect with the people he loved.

Retirement suited Wesley.  He made up for lost time with his wife.  He doted on his grandkids.  He cooked dinner for the whole family every Wednesday.  He was happy.

Then he got the call.

The authorities claimed that it was a freak accident... that the fire consumed his son's house before anyone got a chance to escape.  But Wesley knew better.  His son's wife was an Ixian who could snuff out flame with a thought.  If their bodies were found burned they had to be dead long before there was a fire.

And so Grey Talon has picked up his bow once more, and when he finds who's responsible there will be a bloody reckoning.

## Overview

[edit | edit source]

Grey Talon's motivation in the ritual is to bring retribution to the Friends of Humanity, an extremist neopolitical group who he believes are behind the murder of his family.[1][2][3]

### Biography

[edit | edit source]

Grey Talon was one of the founding members of the Baxter Society, creating the organisation 5-8 years after the first Maelstrom.[4][5]

Grey Talon, while retired, is still heavily respected by the Baxter Society.[6] His dedication to the society resulted in books being made about his life and earning him a reputation as a "legend".[7][8] His dedication to his work, however, meant he was often away from home, which meant he missed watching his son grow up.[9] Refusing to make the same mistake for his grandkids, Grey Talon retired after roughly 40 years of service.[10] When he retired, Grey Talon was said to have more confirmed kills than anyone else in the Baxter Society.[11]

Grey Talon's spirit owl is named Rohan.[12] His son was named Nathan.[13] It's implied that Grey Talon's wife is still alive and wouldn't approve of his quest for revenge.[14]

### Personality

Grey Talon considers his life "hollow".[15]

Most characters respect Grey Talon and dislike fighting him.[16]

Grey Talon has visited South Ixia and said it was "beautiful".[17]

Grey Talon was handsome in his younger years.[18]

## Character Relationships

[edit | edit source]

### Vindicta

[edit | edit source]

Grey Talon and Vindicta have hunted the Friends of Humanity together and regard each other as friends.[19][20][21]

Vindicta calls Grey Talon by his first name and believes that, if her father was like Grey Talon, she would never have died during the Salem Witch Trials.[22]

### Shiv

[edit | edit source]

Grey Talon chose Shiv to act as his replacement in the Baxter Society.[23][24]

Grey Talon has high hopes for Shiv, despite his criminal past, and holds him in high regard, placing Shiv in the top 8 of people he'd want fighting alongside him.[25][26]

One of Shiv's goals in the ritual is to prove his worth to Grey Talon.[27]

Silver can't believe Grey Talon stood up for Shiv.[28]  Venator warns Grey Talon that, if Shiv slips up, he won't be able to protect Shiv from the Swords of Saint Benedict.[29]

### Drifter

[edit | edit source]

Grey Talon and Drifter have a long history with each other.

Drifter refers to Grey Talon by his first name.[30]

While Grey Talon despises Drifter,[31][32] Drifter holds Talon in high regards.[33][34]

Grey Talon suspects Drifter could have killed his family, although Drifter affirms that if he did murder them, he would have made Grey Talon watch.[35]

There was a particularly brutal interaction between the two in Oklahoma.[36]

### Calico

[edit | edit source]

Calico used to work with Grey Talon in the Baxter Society.

While Grey Talon is disappointed in Calico for becoming an assassin,[37] he doesn't resent her and was content to leave her alone.[38][39][40]

Calico views Grey Talon as a "relic of a bygone era",[41] but also says she will mourn his death if she kills him in-game.[42]

### Haze

[edit | edit source]

Haze and Grey Talon know each other. She refers to him by his first name and sent a card when his son Nathan died.

They haven't seen each other for "a while".[43][44]

### Warden

[edit | edit source]

Warden once looked up to Grey Talon and heavily respects him.[45][46]

When on opposing teams, Warden is shocked that Talon would willingly work for the Patrons.[47]

While Grey Talon appreciates Warden's conviction,[48] he believes that both he and Warden live "hollow lives".[15]

## Character Interactions

[edit | edit source]

- Grey Talon respects Dynamo's courage for enduring his body being torn apart and wishes he had the same optimism Dynamo has.[49]
- Abrams and Paige remind Grey Talon of his daughter-in-law.[50][51]
- Kelvin is sorry for Grey Talon, but insists to him that his rage won't bring his family back.[52][53]
- Mo & Krill like Grey Talon for his war against the Friends of Humanity.[54]
- Holliday likes Grey Talon due to their shared goal of revenge.[55]  Calico compares Holliday's introverted personality to Grey Talon's.[56]
- Yamato respects Grey Talon for his age and skill.[57][58]
- Viscous shows concern for Grey Talon's sadness and offers The Cube to him to try and cheer him up.[59]
- Rem refers to Grey Talon as "grandpa" and wants to play with him.[60][61]
- When killing  Calico,  Venator mentions that it was "for Grey Talon".[62]
- While trapped in the Baroness, The Doorman taunts Grey Talon by saying that before his retirement, he prioritized his work in the Baxter Society over his family and "robbed them of a father".[63]
- Billy,[64]  Calico[65], Lady Geist[66], Mina[67],  McGinnis,[68] Silver,[69] Viscous[70] and  Vyper[71] comment on Grey Talon's health, due to his age.
