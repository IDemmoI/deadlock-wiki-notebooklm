## Notes

[edit | edit source]

- Tankbuster is a Charge-Up item, so it's not affected by Cooldown Reduction.
- Tankbuster only goes on cooldown when it affects enemy heroes. It will not go on cooldown when you hit an NPC.
- Some forms of Spirit Amp applied by items count as separate damage instances and thus don't contribute to the single hit such as Escalating Exposure.
- When the target is hit with spirit damage, the damage value which is compared against the triggering threshold is the base damage value prior to any increases or decreases from damage amp, damage reduction, spirit resistance, and spirit resistance reduction.

- Damage amp and spirit resist reduction will not help you in achieving the damage threshold to trigger the passive.
- Damage reduction and the target's spirit resistance will not prevent the passive from triggering when it otherwise would.
- Interaction with spirit shields: The triggering of the additional damage is entirely unaffected by the target's barriers (or lack thereof).
- The additional damage, and the ability which triggered the additional damage, are applied to the target as separate instances of damage. The additional damage from Tankbuster is dealt after the original spell damage.
