# Fletcher Dunn

Fletcher Dunn is a *Deadlock* developer. He is a software engineer at Valve, known for his work on *Counter-Strike: Global Offensive* and *Counter-Strike 2*.

Fletcher worked on the algorithm for *Deadlock's* matchmaking system.[1]
