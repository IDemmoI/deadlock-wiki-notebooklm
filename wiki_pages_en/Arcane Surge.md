**Arcane Surge** is a Tier 2  Spirit Item that can be purchased from the Shop for  **1,600**. Its component,  Extra Stamina, makes up  **800** of the cost.

## Notes

[edit | edit source]

- Items and passive abilities cannot benefit from the conditional buff - they will not gain additional spirit power, duration, or range.
- The buff which provides the player with the conditional ability range, ability duration, and spirit power lasts for 7 seconds.
- The first ability cast after gaining the buff will consume the buff.
- The conditional ability range and ability duration are snapshot on ability use.
- The conditional spirit power is tied to the 7 second timer of the conditional buff. The +20 spirit power will only be available for 7 seconds after the player initially gains the conditional buff.

Examples:

- 6 seconds after gaining the conditional buff,  Vindicta uses  Assassinate.

- This consumes the buff, and applies +20 spirit power to  Assassinate's damage.
- 6 seconds after gaining the conditional buff,  Seven uses  Lightning Ball.

- The buff is consumed.
- The  Lightning Ball gains +15% ability duration and +12% ability range.
- The  Lightning Ball gains +20 spirit power for 1 second only. For the rest of its duration, it does not benefit from +20 spirit power.
- 6 seconds after gaining the conditional buff,  Seven uses  Power Surge.

- The buff is consumed.
- Power Surge gains +15% ability duration and +12% ability range.
- Power Surge's *damage* benefits from +20 spirit power for 1 second only. For the rest of its duration, the *damage* does not benefit from +20 spirit power.
- Power Surge's total *duration* scales natively with spirit power at time of cast. The additional duration due to the +20 spirit power is snapshot on cast, and is not lost when the 7s conditional buff is consumed.
