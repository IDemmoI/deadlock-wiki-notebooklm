**Healing Rite** is a Tier 1  Vitality Item that can be purchased from the Shop for  **800**.

It is a component of  Healing Nova and  Rescue Beam.

## Notes

[edit | edit source]

The heal gets dispelled even if the damage was absorbed by a barrier.
