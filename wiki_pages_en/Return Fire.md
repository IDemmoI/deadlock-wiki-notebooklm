**Return Fire** is a Tier 2  Vitality Item that can be purchased from the Shop for  **1,600**.

## Notes

[edit | edit source]

- **Damage Reflection Mechanics**

- Returns the attacker's **pre-mitigation** damage (original damage before your resistances apply)

- Example: If an enemy deals 100 Bullet Damage while you have 30% Bullet Resist, you reflect 65 damage, and you take 70.
- Works even with 100% damage immunity (e.g.,  Metal Skin reflects full damage while preventing damage to the user).
- Does not apply build-up or stacking effects against the attacker.
