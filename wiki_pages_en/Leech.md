**Leech** is a Tier 4  Vitality Item that can be purchased from the Shop for  **6,400**. Purchasing one of its components,  Bullet Lifesteal or  Spirit Lifesteal, discounts the cost by  **1,600**.
Purchasing both components discounts the cost by  **3200**.
