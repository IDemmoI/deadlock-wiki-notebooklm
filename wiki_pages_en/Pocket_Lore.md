Arin Fairfax, the eldest heir to Fairfax Industries, has been living in hiding for the past 5 years after being shot and left for dead on their 18th birthday.  Arin doesn't know who is trying to keep them from taking control of their parent's company... but in the avarice fueled viper's nest that is Fairfax Industries it could be anyone.

Taking on the name "Pocket", Arin desperately wants to keep their true identity a secret… not only for their own protection; but because they want to forge their own identity separate from their parents.

## Overview

[edit | edit source]

Walter Fairfax statue

Pocket's motivation in the ritual is to stop their father, Maximilian Fairfax,[1][2] and to take control of Fairfax Industries.[3][4]

Pocket believes their father was behind their attempted assasination.[5] Pocket wants to stop their father rather than kill him.[2] Maximilian seems to know that Pocket survived, as in *The Binding of Oathkeeper*, he asks Lady Geist if she knows where Pocket is.[6] Pocket also does not trust their mother.[7]

### Biography

[edit | edit source]

A statue of Walter Fairfax, presumably an ancestor of Pocket, can be found in the garden behind the Baroness Hotel. Walter is depicted as an old man with frogs crawling over his legs. The statue also appears in Pocket's background in the hero select screen.

Pocket likely has at least one younger sibling, as their background calls them the "eldest heir" to Fairfax Industries.

Pocket went to Blackmore Academy.[8]

Pocket was betrothed to Mina, implied to be due to an arranged marriage.[9][10][11]

Calico was the assassin hired to kill Pocket, who survived because the bullet missed their heart.[12] It's implied that Enchanter's Satchel also helped Pocket survive.[13][14] Some time after the assassination, Pocket left New York before returning to participate in the ritual.[15][16]

### Personality

[edit | edit source]

Pocket expresses regret for killing most of their enemies and say they only chose violence as a last resort.[17][18]

### Information

[edit | edit source]

Pocket's magical suitcase, while powerful, was never meant to be used as a weapon.[19] Pocket is trying to keep their suitcase away from Maximilian.[20]

## Character Relationships

[edit | edit source]

### Calico

[edit | edit source]

As the assassin hired to kill Pocket, Calico enjoys "tying up loose ends" when she kills Pocket in-game.[21] She also mentions that Maximilian Fairfax will "have questions".[22] Pocket, naturally, dislikes Calico for almost killing them.[23][24]

### Dynamo

[edit | edit source]

Dynamo is Pocket's godfather. He recognizes Pocket and says he would have helped them if he knew they were alive. Pocket didn't tell Dynamo that they survived because they weren't sure if they could trust him.[25][26]

### Mina

[edit | edit source]

Mina hates Pocket for "ruining her life", as their assassination meant Mina couldn't become a Fairfax.[9][10][27]

### Mo & Krill

[edit | edit source]

Mo & Krill protected Pocket after their assassination, and they regard each other as family.[28][29][30] They love each other.[31]

## Character Interactions

[edit | edit source]

- Despite Pocket's desire to hide their true identity, the other characters seem to be aware of their background and refer to them as an "upstart",[32] a "trust fund baby"[33] and a "spoiled brat".[34]
- Pocket loathes McGinnis due to her work for Fairfax Industries.[35][36][37]
- Pocket thinks  Yamato would get along with their mother.[38]
- Pocket says Vyper is more unstable than their parents[39] and is relieved when she is killed, even when both are on the same team.[40]
- Lady Geist recognises Pocket, but mistakes them for a street urchin. She thinks Pocket's jacket is stolen.[41]
- Bebop offers Pocket a place to stay after the ritual.[42]
- Graves, Paradox and Yamato want Pocket's case.[43][44][45]

- Warden warns Pocket that their case will be their undoing.[46]
- According to  Rem, Pocket's clothes smell like the sewer.[47]
