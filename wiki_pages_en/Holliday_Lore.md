There are people who spent their whole lives dreaming about moving to New York.  Holliday isn't one of those people.  Content with being a Sheriff in a small town, Holliday enjoyed a career where the only time she had to use her gun was when trick shooting at the county fair...then the murders started.

Grisly and inhumane; the bodies were found with their ribcages ripped open... their innards picked apart as if by birds.  Holliday tracked down the killer, a travelling folk musician that the media dubbed "The Troubadour", but when she confronted him he exploded into a murder of crows and flew away.

That was 6 months ago, and while the murders in central Illinois have stopped, Holliday's investigation has not.  She believes that The Troubadour is travelling to New York, and when she finds him she's going to kill that evil son of a bitch.

## Overview

[edit | edit source]

Holliday's motivation and wish in the ritual is to catch and kill the Troubadour.[1][2][3][4]

### Biography

[edit | edit source]

Holliday's hometown is Macomb, Illinois.[5][6]

Holliday is the granddaughter of Wild West gunslinger Doc Holliday.[7][8][9] Her revolver belonged to her grandfather and is seen as a "family heirloom".[10][11] She inherited her revolver from her father, who also trained her to use it.[12]

Holliday is vigilant as a law enforcer and takes her job seriously.[13][14] The OSIC have a file on her, where she's portrayed as "competent."[15]

Holliday is a Christian.[16]

Holliday's visual novel will tell the story of her hunt for the Troubadour.

### Personality

[edit | edit source]

Holliday was traumatised by the Troubadour's murders.[17] She avoids sleeping because of the nightmares she gets.[18] Holliday hates the feeling of revenge, but feels like there's nothing else she can do about it.[19]

Holliday doesn't like big cities like New York or Chicago.[20][21][22] Holliday is described as a tourist,[23] but she has no intention of sightseeing.[24][25] Holliday wants to leave New York as soon as possible.[26][27]

Holliday feels pressured by Doc Holliday's legacy and tries not to think about it,[9] but hopes her actions would make him proud.[28][29]

### Information

[edit | edit source]

Holliday had a sister who died.[30]

Holliday has been married more than once,[31] and she doesn't want to have children.[32]

Holliday's boots were a gift.[33]

### Captain Murphy

[edit | edit source]

Captain Murphy is an old colleague of Hollidays.

In *Holliday's visual novel*, Captain Murphy's receptionist spreads a rumor that Holliday wants to have an affair with Murphy, who is married.[34]  Haze and Krill tease Holliday about the rumor.[35][36] Yamato believes the rumor and threatens Murphy's life to keep Holliday in line.[37]

### Venator

[edit | edit source]

Venator prays with Holliday before the ritual to try and help heal her trauma from the Macomb massacre. While it doesn't work, Holliday appreciates Venator for trying.[16]

Venator also warns Holliday to tend her pain and anger before it consumes her.[38]

## Character Interactions

[edit | edit source]

- Most enemies express sympathy towards Holliday.[39]
- Holliday mistakes  Viscous' home, The Deep, for the American Deep South. She asks if he's from Mobile, Alabama.[40]
- Holliday likes  Grey Talon due to their shared goal of revenge.[41]
- Holliday threatens to stay in New York to take down  Lady Geist,  Vindicta and  Wraith for their crimes.[42][43][44]
- Holliday thinks Warden has issues and calls him a "creepy homeschooled kid."[45][46] If they're on opposing teams, however, Holliday wishes that they were working together.[47]
- Holliday mistakes Krill for Mo.[48]
- The Troubadour is unaware that Holliday is after him. Drifter warns Holliday that, if the Troubadour found out Holliday was after him, he'd see Holliday as his "hobby". Drifter tells Holliday to let go of her vengeance for her own sake.[49]
- As a sheriff, Holliday is antagonized by criminals like  Wraith and  Yamato.[39]
- Shiv respects Holliday and offers her a place at the Baxter Society.[50] If they're on opposing teams, Shiv promises to kill the Troubadour on Holliday's behalf.[51]
- If they're on the opposing team, Ivy,[52] Shiv[53] and Warden[54] tell Holliday that they could've worked together to stop the Troubadour.
- Infernus invites Holliday for a "date" at Jezebel's.[55]
- Lash attempts to flirt with Holliday.[56][57]
- Calico compares Holliday's introverted personality to  Grey Talon's.[58]
- Ivy wants to go sightseeing with Holliday.[59]
- Mina thinks Holliday is old.[60]
- Mirage warns Holliday that her blind quest for revenge will get her killed.[61]
- Paradox wants to steal Holliday's revolver.[62]
- Silver is after a bounty on the Troubadour and wants Holliday to stay out of her way.[63]
- Lash,[64] Mina,[65] Shiv,[66]  Viscous[67] and Vyper[68] like Holliday's hat.
- Lady Geist thinks Holliday's clothes are "horrible."[69]
- Apollo,[70] Mirage[71] and Paige[72] think Holliday isn't in the right state of mind to be a cop.
