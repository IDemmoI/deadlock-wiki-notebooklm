##  How this AI interprets Game Stats (Spirit Scaling & Tiers)
To ensure 100% accurate mathematical and statistical answers, this notebook includes a companion file called `deadlock_wiki_explanation.md`. It acts as a "decryption key" for the AI, teaching it how to read custom wiki notations:
*   **Spirit Scaling (`*x*Multiplier` / `xMultiplier`)**: If you see `*x*1.3`, it means the stat gains an additional `1.3` value for every `1` point of Spirit Power.
    *   *Formula*: `Total = Base + (Spirit Power * Multiplier)`.
*   **Upgrade Tiers (`1`, `2`, `5`)**: These represent Tier 1 (costs 1 AP), Tier 2 (costs 2 AP), and Tier 3 (costs 5 AP) upgrades for abilities, along with their respective bonuses.
*   **Level Growth (`Base+Growth` Syntax)**: Stats like Bullet Damage written as `18.5+0.825` mean `18.5` base damage at Level 1, growing by `0.825` with each hero level.
