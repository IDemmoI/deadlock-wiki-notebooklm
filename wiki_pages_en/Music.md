## Overview

[edit | edit source]

*Deadlock's* main musical identity since the Six New Heroes update displays elements of Trip hop, a genre popularized in the 1990s and defined as a psychedelic fusion of hip-hop and electronica, with influences from jazz and other styles.

## Composer

[edit | edit source]

The music for *Deadlock* was composed by a member of the development team who wishes to remain anonymous for the time being. In a private email, Valve composer Mike Morasky explained that the composer will likely take credit at a future date and confirmed it was not him or "anyone else you might normally guess."[1] Morasky had also previously denied his involvement publicly on X (formerly Twitter).[2]
