# Municipal Coven

“

The city owes you boys in the Municipal Coven a lot.

— Shopkeeper

The Municipal Coven makes sure the streets of New York stay safe and tidy.

## Overview

[edit | edit source]

The New York Municipal Coven works as the primary caretakers of New York.

During the festive season, the Municipal Coven were looking for volunteers to spend time with stray familiars in their Soho shelter. [1]

## Removed Lore

[edit | edit source]

Trapper is a Municipal Coven employee. His duties include building Spirit Urns, luring giant rats, and capturing invasive species from other realms.[2] Staff were given the option to work on Maelstrom day, which Trapper volunteered to do.[3]

Trapper believes the Municipal Coven needs to be respected more.[4]

Holliday[5] and the Shopkeeper[6] respect what the Municipal Coven does for the city.

The Municipal Coven seems to accept donations.[7]
