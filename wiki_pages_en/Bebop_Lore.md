Forged by the hands of a widowed junk yard attendant; Bebop was more than just some scrap golem laborer... he was the son she couldn't have.  Together they would work in the iron yard during the day, and listen to jazz at night. It was a predictable, if not comforting routine.  But now, Bebop's creator has fallen ill and while Miss Shelly wouldn't approve of the activities that Bebop does at night to pay for her medicine, what she doesn't know can't hurt her.

## Overview

[edit | edit source]

Bebop's motivation and wish in the ritual is to cure Ms. Shelly of her illness.[1][2]

### Biography

[edit | edit source]

Bebop describes his creation as "an act of craftsmanship, willpower and sacrifice".[3] Bebop seems to have a soul.[4]

Bebop's main source of income for Ms. Shelly's medicine is by fighting in the Bear Pit.[5][6][7] Once Ms. Shelly is cured of her illness, he intends to retire.[8]

### Personality

[edit | edit source]

Bebop was created with access to his own free will.[9] While Bebop doesn't particularly value human life,[10][11] he knows Ms. Shelly wouldn't approve of him killing people.[12][13]

### Information

[edit | edit source]

Bebop can speak French, but can't speak Japanese.[14][15]

Bebop calls humans "organics".[16][17]

Bebop knows how to bake biscuits.[18]

## Character Relationships

[edit | edit source]

### Shelly Fisher

[edit | edit source]

The creator of Bebop.

Bebop refers to Ms. Shelly as his "mother".[19]

Bebop offers to introduce Ms. Shelly to  Drifter,[20]  Victor[21] and  Viscous.[22] He also wanted  McGinnis to speak to her.[23]

### Lash

[edit | edit source]

Bebop and Lash have a rivalry fueled by hate for each other.[24][25][26]

Lash used to use an uppercut instead of Ground Strike, but refused to use it ever again after seeing Bebop use an uppercut.[27][28]

Bebop has beaten Lash in the Bear Pit once,[29] and Lash has beaten Bebop at least once afterwards.[30][31]

Neither Bebop nor Lash elaborate on why they hate the other so much.[32][33]

Lash has at least 87 reasons why he's better than Bebop.[34]

-Wraith + M&K

## Character Interactions

[edit | edit source]

- Bebop offers  Pocket a place to stay after the ritual.[35]
- Lady Geist regards Bebop as a man of culture after learning he speaks French.[36]
- Ivy invites Bebop and Ms. Shelly to visit the Arroyo's for dinner.[37]
- Mo & Krill watch Bebop's fights in the Bear Pit and consistently bet on him winning.[38] They know Ms. Shelly and have tried to help her.[39]
- Vyper is a fan of  Lash and dislikes Bebop.[40][41]
- Wraith criticizes Bebop for not telling Ms. Shelly about his work as a pitfighter.[42]
- McGinnis and  Paradox are impressed by the craftsmanship used to create Bebop.[43][44]
- Seven believes Bebop's love for Ms. Shelly makes him weak. Seven threatens that, if he considered Bebop a threat, he would go after Ms. Shelly to get to Bebop.[45]
