**Capacitor** is a Tier 4  Weapon Item that can be purchased from the Shop for  **6,400**. Its component,  Tesla Bullets, makes up  **3,200** of the cost.

## Notes

[edit | edit source]

- Capacitor has an internal cooldown of 0.25 seconds, which is lowered by item cooldown reduction.
- Shock Damage is considered Spirit Damage and is reduced by Spirit Resist.
