Even in a world of magic, some things are still rare and unusual. So what is a unicorn to do?  Announce her presence to the world and potentially have poachers looking to saw off her horn? Or hide in plain sight at a Coney Island Side Show where most folk would assume her appearance was just part of an act?

Over the years "The Dazzling Celeste" has grown into one of the more popular attractions on Coney, and while the applause from a cheering audience is appreciated; what keeps her waking up in the morning is the found family she's made at work.

Keruba the Mermaid.

Blockhead Lenny.

Chickenwing.

Ted.

These were her people.  So when she discovered that one of her own sold part of their soul in order to cover a gambling debt, Celeste knew it was time to step out of the shadows and do whatever it takes to get it back.  Is revealing her power to The Patrons a risk?  Yes.  But that's tomorrow's problem... her family needs help today.

## Overview

[edit | edit source]

### Biography

[edit | edit source]

Celeste's existence as a unicorn is questioned by skeptics who don't believe unicorns are real.[1][2][3] Some of them believe that she glued a horn to her head.[4] Only her closest friends know her true identity.[5]

Celeste has frequently caused accidents when performing or training her acts.[6][7] She used to close kids' birthday parties with her Light Eater ability until she faced multiple lawsuits.[8] She also took Shining Wonder out of her act after causing too many concussions.[9]

### Personality

[edit | edit source]

Celeste hates hurting other people, expressing remorse when killing enemies.[10][11] She doesn't like when "the pretty ones" try to kill her.[12]

### Information

[edit | edit source]

The Paradox Organisation have tried to steal Celeste's horn.[13]

## Character Relationships

[edit | edit source]

### Blockhead Lenny

[edit | edit source]

Blockhead Lenny is one of Celeste's found family members.

Celeste dislikes  Lash for beating Lenny in the Bear Pit.[14]

It's implied that Lenny is the friend who sold his soul.[15]

### Mo & Krill

[edit | edit source]

Celeste is close friends with Mo and Krill.[16]

While on opposing teams, Celeste says people like her and Mo were supposed to stick together.[17] Additionally, Mo wants to cry if he kills Celeste.[18]

Krill tells Celeste that she doesn't need to spend her life hiding in a circus.[19]

### Infernus and Hank

[edit | edit source]

Infernus is friends with Celeste and her found family. Celeste invites Infernus to come over to say hi to her family at the sideshow.[20]

Celeste refers to Infernus as "Fern".[21] She's also familiar with Hank and asks Infernus how Hank is holding up.[22]

### Wraith

[edit | edit source]

Wraith has a marker on someone named "Dutch", who seems to be a friend of Celeste as she demands Wraith forgets the marker.[23]

While Celeste isn't fond of Wraith, she does think she's "pretty charming".[24]

## Character Interactions

[edit | edit source]

- Celeste compliments  Calico's voice,[25]  Haze's tie and eyes,[26][27]  Lady Geist's hair and necklace[28][29] and  Mina's dress.[30] She finds Geist "pretty",[12]  Paige "adorable",[31]  Silver a "hot mess",[32] and  Wraith "charming".[24]
- Celeste offers to have a drink with  Silver after the ritual.[33]
- Celeste offers free tickets to  Bebop, Ms. Shelly and  Mirage to see her show.[34][35]
- Celeste "doesn't get the appeal" of  Shiv and says he has "ruined a lot of lives".[36][37]
- Celeste mistakes  Grey Talon's name as "Gary Talon".[38][39]
- Seven thinks it's pathetic that Celeste hides her true power to work at the circus.[40]
- According to  Rem, Celeste smells like peppermint.[41]

## Removed Lore

[edit | edit source]

Celeste was named **Pepper** during early development.[42]
