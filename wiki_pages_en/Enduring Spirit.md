# Enduring Spirit

“Farewell, my friends.”

This page contains game content that has been removed from the game.

**Enduring Spirit** was a Tier 1 Vitality Item that provided Bonus Health, Spirit Lifesteal and Spirit Power to the purchasing hero.

It could be purchased for  **500** from the Shop.

Enduring Spirit was a component of Slowing Hex.
