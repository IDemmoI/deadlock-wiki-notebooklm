# Jamaal Bradley

**Jamaal Bradley** is a *Deadlock* developer, known for his animation work on *Left 4 Dead 2, Team Fortress 2, Half-Life: Alyx* and other Valve titles and animated movies.

Jamaal is an animator on *Deadlock* and has shared an animation test of McGinnis.[1]
