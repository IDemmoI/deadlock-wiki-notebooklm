**Tesla Bullets** is a Tier 3  Weapon Item that can be purchased from the Shop for  **3,200**.

It is a component of  Capacitor.

## Notes

[edit | edit source]

- Tesla Bullets have an internal cooldown of 0.25 seconds, which is lowered by item cooldown reduction.
- Shock Damage is considered Spirit Damage and is reduced by Spirit Resist.

## Trivia

[edit | edit source]

- Tesla Bullets is one of several items to feature in promotional posters around the Map.
- Tesla Bullets is named after Serbian-American inventor Nikola Tesla.
