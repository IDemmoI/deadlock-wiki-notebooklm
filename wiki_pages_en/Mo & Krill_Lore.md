Mo and Krill are members of The Tunnel Rats, a group that help facilitate illicit trade and commerce between the boroughs thanks to a network of underground tunnels.  But while most denizens of the New York underworld respect the work The Rats do, every once in a while a gang tries to exploit the tunnel system for personal vendettas...that's when Mo and Krill step in and remind the city what happens to those who disrespect their hospitality.

## Overview

[edit | edit source]

Mo & Krill's motivation in the ritual is to achieve the respect and glory they believe is owed to them.[1][2]

### Biography

[edit | edit source]

Mo is a mole man.[3][4] Krill's species is currently unknown, but he has monkey-like characteristics. He also seems to have fur.[5]

Mo and Krill have been forced to live in the margins of society.[6] It's unknown how the two met, but Krill mentions that their friendship was forged from "respect and certain death".[7]

Mo's full name is Maurice.[8] Krill and Pocket also calls him "Momo".[9][10] Mo is learning pottery and wants to audit pottery classes at Columbia University,[11][12] although it's implied that he doesn't know to use a pottery wheel.[13]

Krill is an author and is writing a memoir about his and Mo's adventures, which currently has 31 chapters.[14][11]

### Personality

[edit | edit source]

Mo & Krill value their independence.[15] They're friends with  Wraith and used to do business with the Mendoza Syndicate.[16][17][18]

Mo & Krill don't like the Friends of Humanity.[19] They also don't like it when their names get mixed up.[20]

Mo knows how to speak, but doesn't speak around people he's unfamiliar with.[21][22]

### Information

[edit | edit source]

Mo stinks.[23][24] Krill has bathed in sewer sludge.[25]

Mo & Krill have smuggled a wide variety of objects in the New York tunnels, such as jewels, fine art and Ixian trinkets.[26] They also offer information for a "reasonable price",[27] and accept money to dig new underground tunnels.[28] Notable items of value they've sold include trinkets from  Kelvin's Ixia expedition and knock-offs of Mina's dresses.[29][30] Krill knows someone looking to sell a first edition copy of "*Don Quixote".*[31]

Mo & Krill don't pay taxes.[32]

## Character Relationships

[edit | edit source]

### Pocket

[edit | edit source]

Mo & Krill protected Pocket after Pocket's assassination attempt, and they regard each other as family.[33][34][35] They love each other.[36]

### Wraith

[edit | edit source]

Mo & Krill are in Wraith's weekly poker game and owe money to her.[37] If they're on the same team, Wraith may say she called a favor to have them fighting on her side.[17] They regard each other as friends.[16][38]

## Character Interactions

[edit | edit source]

- Mo & Krill watch and bet on  Bebop's fights in the Bear Pit.[39] They know Ms. Shelly and have tried to help her.[40]
- Mo & Krill are friends with Infernus and have offered to help him prevent Jezebel's from closing.[41]
- Mo & Krill are friends with Abrams.[42][43] Abrams owes Mo & Krill $50.[37]
- Krill compares Apollo to a teenage version of Lash.[44]
- Krill is impressed by  Lady Geist's ward and Holliday's revolver.[45][46]
- Krill has read  Kelvin's books and found them "riveting".[14]
- Krill liked Sinclair's stage show and pities them for their fate.[47]
- Mo wants to cry if he kills Celeste. [48]
- Lady Geist shows contempt and disgust towards Mo & Krill.[49][50]
- Yamato wants to turn Mo into a coat and Krill into a hat.[51]
- Seven hides in Mo & Krill's subway tunnels, without their permission.[52]
- Mendoza was a "good" client of Mo & Krill.[18]
- Vyper really likes Mo & Krill.[53] They don't like her.[54]
- Paige likes Mo & Krill and wants to be their friend.[55] Krill mentions that they once planned to start a book club together.[56]
- While trapped inside the Baroness, The Doorman attempts to split Mo & Krill up from each other. He tells Mo that Krill is dead weight and that he should kill him, and he tells Krill that Mo is keeping him down and would be better off if he never met Krill.[57]  Seven also tells Mo that Krill is holding him back.[58]

- When killing the Doorman, Krill reassures Mo that he'd never betray him.[59]
- Kelvin,[7] Paige,[60] Vindicta[61] respect Mo & Krill's friendship.
- Dynamo,[62] Holliday,[63] Ivy[64] and Lash[65] don't know which of the duo is Mo or Krill.
- The Shopkeeper and Rem want to hug Mo.[66][67]

## Removed Lore

[edit | edit source]

In a removed voice line,  McGinnis referred to Krill as a rat.[68]
