**Damage Amplification** or **Damage Amp** is a stat that multiplies the damage caused by the player to affected enemies and NPCs.

## Damage Reduction

[edit | edit source]

**Damage Reduction** is a negative form of Damage Amplification, that reduces the damage output of enemies or its user.
