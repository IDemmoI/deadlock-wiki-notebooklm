All characters have the ability to Sprint. Sprinting in **Deadlock** is a passive action — no further action is required to sprint. When sprinting, you instantly accelerate up to +2.5 m/s of your base sprint speed[1]), then gain 0.6 m/s every second[2]) above that threshold. Sprinting characters can be identified by the grey speed lines coming off them. You cannot sprint while zooming in using alt-fire).

Every character has a base sprint speed of 1.6 m/s except:

- Bebop – 4 m/s
- Kelvin – 1.1 m/s
- Lady Geist – 2.4 m/s
- Lash – 2.1 m/s
- Paige – 3.5 m/s
- Victor – 1.1 m/s

When characters are sprinting:

- If they have been damaged by an enemy hero, sprint will be disabled for 3 seconds, which can be significantly slower.
- If they have been damaged by a non-hero, sprint will be disabled for 3 seconds).[3]
- If they are firing, they will move significantly slower (around ~70% of their base walk speed) until the character stops firing.

- With the  Weapon Item  Fleetfoot, they will move at their base walk speed if they are firing.
- Ivy’s  Kudzu Connection, provides Fleetfoot's passive to both her and the tethered teammate.

#### Gaining Sprint Speed from Runes

[edit | edit source]

Main article: Runes

Sprint speed can be obtained by picking up the Movement Rune Movement Rune above the bridges between the inner and outer lanes. The Movement Rune Movement rune grants a temporary base +3 m/s sprint speed buff for 160 seconds, that scales with time.
