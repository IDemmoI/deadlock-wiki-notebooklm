“

If they don't have an Achilles' heel, I'll make one.

**Vindicta** is a Hero in *Deadlock*. She is a mobile and powerful marksman, engaging and picking off her targets from a distance. Using her Crow Familiar to initiate fights by bleeding her targets, Vindicta may launch into Flight to keep her distance from enemies and tether them with a Stake in the ground while targeting them with her powerful rifle. Enemies that are near-death should fear her ability to Assassinate at long-range with a devastating sniper shot.

A girl who was accused of witchcraft and killed by John Hathorne during the Salem Witch Trials of the 1600s, Vindicta returned to this world during the first Maelstrom. Seeking to protect her fellow supernatural entities, Vindicta is waging a war against the Neo-inquisitorial "Friends of Humanity".

## Backstory

[edit | edit source]

*See **Vindicta/Lore** for more story content.*

“

A victim of John Hathorne during the Salem Witch Trials, Vindicta got a second chance of life when her spirit was brought back to the mortal plane during the first Maelstrom (the event that introduced the supernatural to the public at large).

Waging a one ghost war against "The Friends of Humanity", a neo inquisition formed by Hathorne's descendants; Vindicta looks to keep the supernatural safe from Hathorne's ilk, and she isn't afraid to stack as many bodies as needed to do it.

## Trivia

[edit | edit source]

- Vindicta was named **Hornet** in *Neon Prime*.

- Hornet was voiced by Jeannie Tirado, who also voices Ivy.
- Vindicta's current model was designed by artist Evgeniy Evstrastiy.[1]
- Vindicta will occasionally hum the song *Simple Gifts* after using a shot of her ultimate.[2] This is a song that originated from the Shaker religion in the early 1800s.
- "Brooding in the Hideout" is displayed as one's status in the in-game Friends List when playing as Vindicta in the Hideout.
