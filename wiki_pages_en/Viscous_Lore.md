The Bermuda Triangle was never a myth, it was a shadowline nexus deep underneath the Atlantic Ocean.  For the most part, the things that escaped this astral gate were benign (like Nessie), but now something dark and predatory known only as The Adversary hunts the seas... and ocean denizens are scared to death.

One of those inhabitants, a psychic sea anemone, wants to do something about it.  Summoning an ectoplasmic goo body to traverse the surface, Viscous is desperately trying to summon the Patrons in order to keep The Deep safe from the horror that lurks below.

### Personality

[edit | edit source]

Viscous is innocent and optimistic, eager to learn about the surface world and make new friends, and likes to compliment others.[1] However, he takes his mission to stop the Adversary very seriously, as noted by  Seven.[2] He believes that others underestimate him and the problems of the Deep.[3][4]

Viscous is commonly referred to as a "tourist".[5][6][7][8] While on the surface, he wants to watch South Pacific,[9] free his "brothers" from New York's aquariums,[10] try climbing some stairs in his goo body,[11] and visit a variety of locations in New York, including Staten Island, Long Island, New Jersey and Secaucus.[12]

Viscous enjoys violence, which surprises him.[13][14]

Viscous likes people-watching.[15]

### Information

[edit | edit source]

Viscous can't read.[16]

The Cube is a protoplasmic cube.[17] The Cube nullifies all pain, even for characters like Victor.[18] Viscous doesn't question how the Cube works, even when it can heal non-organic bodies like Bebop's.[19] Viscous spends time in the Cube whenever he feels sad.[20]

## Character Relationships

[edit | edit source]

### Haze

[edit | edit source]

Haze takes Viscous and his problems seriously.[4] She wants to know more about the Adversary in case more creatures like it come through the Deep's astral gate.[21]

## Character Interactions

[edit | edit source]

- Viscous offers the Cube to Ms. Shelly and Grey Talon.[22][20]
- Viscous likes Holliday's hat and wants to wear it.[23]
- Viscous thinks Seven and Warden are scary.[24][25]
- When  Seven learns about Viscous' mission, he admits there's more to Viscous than he expected.[2]
- Abrams,[26] Bebop, Holliday, Lash, Mina, Mo & Krill and Paradox refer to Viscous as "goo boy".[27]
- Grey Talon offers to have The Baxter Society help Viscous defeat the Adversary should they fail the ritual.[28]
- Holliday mistakes Viscous' home, The Deep, for the American Deep South. She asks if he's from Mobile, Alabama.[29]
- Lady Geist and  McGinnis want to salvage some of Viscous' goo for themselves.[30][31]
- Calico is interested in Viscous' suit.[32]
- Paige wants to take Viscous home and give him a hug.[33]
- Infernus advises Viscous to decline  Wraith's offers to play cards.[34]
- Apollo accidentally invites Viscous to one of his father's galas.[35]

## Removed Lore

Neon Prime
In Neon Prime, Viscous looked the same as the first version of his current model, but he had an icon in which he was depicted with a pufferfish inside his head.
