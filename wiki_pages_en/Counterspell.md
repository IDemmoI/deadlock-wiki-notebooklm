## Notes

[edit | edit source]

- Counterspell is visually indicated by a purple bubble VFX replacing the default parry effect.
- Counterspell only goes on cooldown on a successful spell parry.
- The player can parry multiple abilities during the Counterspell parry window. The heal and buffs are only applied for the first parry.
- Like regular parries, Counterspell has a short grace period where the parry procs after the parry animation has finished.

## Notable interactions

[edit | edit source]

- Counterspell prevents all damage from a Walker stomp and Shrine defense, but does not prevent the stun and knockback. It also does not prevent the Shrine's Curse status effect.
- Counterspell prevents all damage and Curse effect from the Patron's defensive attack.
- Counterspell does not proc when parrying  Viscous'  Puddle Punch, since the ability can be blocked by regular parries.
- Counterspell can prevent  Lash's  Death Slam if the player parries at the moment they would be pulled.
- Counterspell can be used on  The Doorman's  Hotel Guest.
- Counterspell can be used on  Celeste's  Shining Wonder. However, the orb will continue to linger on the player and can bounce onto other heroes.
- Counterspell can prevent Build-Up effects such as  Mina's  Love Bites and stacking effects such as  Haze's  Fixation, if activated either before the build-up is applied or when it triggers. If build-up/stacks have already been applied, it does not prevent the build-up itself.
- Counterspell can prevent  Mirage's  Djinn's Mark before it's applied to the player. However, it does not prevent it from stacking or triggering once it has been applied.
- Counterspell can block instant abilities such as  Mo & Krill's  Combo if the enemy tries to use the ability during the target's parry window, similarly to  Unstoppable.
