## Overview

[edit | edit source]

Wraith's motivation for being in the ritual isn't clear, but it seems to be for a chance to get more wealth, popularity and power.[1][2][3] Wraith mentions that she wants to keep her wish as simple as possible to avoid loopholes.[4]

### Biography

[edit | edit source]

Wraith hosts weekly underground poker games.[5][6][7] She also makes bets on fights in the Bear Pit.[8]

Wraith owns a casino, which acts as her main base of operations.[9][10] As part of her business, Wraith lets customers take out markers from her. Her plan usually involves gambling with people until they owe her money, which forces them to take out markers.[11] Wraith claims to have built her criminal organisation herself, rather than inheriting it.[12]

Wraith has done business with the Mendoza Syndicate in the past,[13] although she dislikes Mendoza himself.[14] Wraith shows interest in working with the Seventh Moon,[15] but ultimately views them as a rival organisation.[16][17] The OSIC are aware of Wraith's business, but aren't interested in dismantling it.[10] Wraith has bribed some of the New York police to leave her business alone,[18][19] and she isn't concerned about Captain Murphy.[20]

Wraith employs Ivy's sister, Sophia Arroyo, to work for Wraith as an accountant to finance her college tuition.[21][22][23] Wraith makes Sophia work long shifts, which makes Sophia tired and affects her academic studies.[24]

### Personality

[edit | edit source]

Wraith describes herself as a "degenerate gambler",[25] "a female entrepreneur in a male-dominated industry" and "Florence Nightingale with a gun".[26] Wraith loves gambling because the nerves she gets from it make her feel alive.[27] She often offers to make bets with her teammates.[28][29]

### Information

[edit | edit source]

Wraith has a long list of people she wants dead.[30]

Wraith wants to get an introduction to Maximilian Fairfax and Apollo's father.[31][32]

## Character Relationships

[edit | edit source]

### Infernus

[edit | edit source]

Wraith and Infernus have worked together in the past.[33] Wraith believes Infernus' work as a bartender is "boring".[34] She believes Infernus misses her, but he denies it.[35] While on the same team, Wraith tries to coax Infernus back into his criminal past.[36][37]

Infernus tells  Mo & Krill that he wants to stay away from Wraith.[38]

According to Graves, Wraith told Blackmore Academy students that play cards with her about Infernus' criminal past "with the mob".[39]

### Mo & Krill

[edit | edit source]

Mo & Krill play in Wraith's card game and owe money to her.[40][41] If they're on the same team, Wraith may say she called a favor to have them fighting on her side.[42] They regard each other as friends.[43][44]

### Ivy

[edit | edit source]

Ivy is distrustful of Wraith. She warns her not to approach Spanish Harlem now that the Mendoza Syndicate has been defeated.[45] Ivy doesn't know that her sister, Sophia, works for Wraith.[21][22]

## Character Interactions

[edit | edit source]

- Wraith has markers on  Mo & Krill,[42] the Shopkeeper,[46] and a talented haberdasher named Jermaine.[47] She also has a marker on Vyper,[48] although Wraith doesn't let her take out markers anymore since Vyper never pays them back.[49]

- Wraith also has a marker on someone named "Dutch", who seems to be a friend of Celeste as she demands Wraith forgets the marker.[50]
- Wraith shows interest in getting  Bebop,[51] Pocket,[52] and Vindicta[30] to work for her.
- Abrams,[5]  Infernus,[6]  Mo & Krill,[40] Silver[53] and the Shopkeeper[54][55] are regular players at Wraith's weekly poker game.
- Lash mocks Wraith for betting on  Bebop,[56] and Wraith teases Lash because he lost to Bebop "one time".[8]
- Yamato views Wraith as her main hurdle towards conquering New York.[57] She hopes to take over Wraith's business.[58]
- Dynamo warns Wraith not to take advantage of his students who frequent her establishment.[9]
- The Doorman thinks Wraith is a "delight" and is grateful towards her since she's a generous tipper.[59][60]
- Holliday hates Wraith and threatens to stay in New York to take down her criminal organisation.[61][62][20]
- Lady Geist says Wraith "reeks of new money". However, she respects Wraith for her cunning and ruthlessness.[47]
- Calico and Paradox show interest in working with Wraith.[63][64][65]
- Haze views Wraith as "a worthy target."[66]
