## Overview

[edit | edit source]

Calico's motivation in the ritual is vague, but she seems to want to be the "Apex Predator" by wishing for power and protection.[1][2]

### Biography

[edit | edit source]

Calico has been an assassin for at least 5 years, as she was hired to kill  Pocket 5 years before the events of the ritual.[3] Pocket survived the assassination attempt due Calico's bullet missing their heart.[4] It's implied that this failed assassination earned Calico a reputation for "sloppy work" amongst other assassins.[5]

Calico is nicknamed as "the most dangerous woman on the planet".[6][7] She's currently being hunted by the International Police,[8] Scotland Yard,[9] and the OSIC.[10][11] She once assassinated someone named "Siegfried" under  Mirage's nose.[12]

Calico has a son.[13]

### Personality

[edit | edit source]

Calico is very knowledgeable, knowing things about the Deep,[14][15] the Troubadour,[16] and Oathkeeper.[17] She's implied to know something about  Victor's origins.[18]

### Information

[edit | edit source]

Calico learned  Return to Shadows while she was in the Baxter Society.[19]

## Character Relationships

[edit | edit source]

### Ava

[edit | edit source]

Ava is Calico's cat familiar.[20]

Calico adores Ava and refers to herself as "mummy" when talking to Ava.[21][22]

It's implied that Calico makes biscuits for Ava to eat.[23]

### Pocket and Maximilian Fairfax

[edit | edit source]

As mentioned in the biography, Calico was the assassin sent to kill Pocket 5 years before the events of the ritual.[3]

Calico enjoys "tying up loose ends" when she kills Pocket in-game.[24] She also mentions that Maximilian Fairfax will "have questions",[25] lining up with Pocket's belief that Maximilian Fairfax was the one who sent Calico to kill Pocket.

Pocket, naturally, dislikes Calico for almost killing them.[26][27]

### Grey Talon

[edit | edit source]

Calico used to work with Grey Talon in the Baxter Society.

While Grey Talon is disappointed in Calico for becoming an assassin,[28] he doesn't resent her and was content to leave her alone.[29][30][31]

Calico views Grey Talon as a "relic of a bygone era",[32] but also says she will mourn his death if she kills him in-game.[33]

### Venator

[edit | edit source]

Venator knew Calico from when she was in the Baxter Society, but loathes her now that she's an assassin.[34]

Venator believes that, for the right price, Calico would be willing to kill Ava.[35]

When killing Calico, Venator mentions that it was "for Grey Talon".[36] He also references the 30 pieces of silver that Judas received for betraying Jesus when killing Calico, presumably referring to Calico leaving the Baxter Society.[37]

### The Doorman

[edit | edit source]

The Doorman is familiar with Calico and likes her.[38][39] He talks with her about "professional interests in town", likely meaning that Calico told him about her job as an assassin.[40]

The Doorman loves Ava and, in the event that Calico will be unable to take care of Ava anymore, says he'll treat her "like a queen" at the Baroness Hotel.[41][42]

## Character Interactions

[edit | edit source]

- Most characters like Ava,[43] with the main exception of Billy.[44]

- Vyper tries to buy Ava with a "crisp Lincoln" ($5).[45]
- Calico has respect for  Paradox.[46] She also loves the Paradox Museum.[47]
- Calico wants to be friends with  Wraith.[48][49]
- Calico compares Holliday's introverted personality to Grey Talon's.[50]
- Mirage believes Calico is beyond redemption.[51]
- Haze has been ordered to kill Calico. If they're on the same team, Haze says she's going to kill Calico before the day is out.[52]

## Removed Lore

[edit | edit source]

Nano (Neon Prime)

When Calico was first added to Hero Labs on October 24, 2024, she originally had a different background:

“

Granted the dubious honor of being named Time Magazine's most dangerous woman on the planet, Calico is an enigmatic occult arms dealer that is willing to fund any and all sides of a conflict if the price is right. 
While her critics (and friends) would correctly identify her as being amoral, she is not without her principles... however cold and calculated those principles may be.

Calico's lore was updated when she officially officially released on January 17, 2025 to her current lore.
