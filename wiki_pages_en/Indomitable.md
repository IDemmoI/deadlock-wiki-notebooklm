**Indomitable** is a Tier 4  Vitality Item that can be purchased from the Shop for  **6,400**. Its component,  Reactive Barrier, makes up  **1,600** of the cost.

## Notable Interactions

[edit | edit source]

- Indomitable will prevent the player from being teleported to The Baroness if hit with  The Doorman's  Hotel Guest.
- Indomitable will not be triggered by  Dynamo's  Singularity.
- Indomitable is currently not triggered by  Apollo's  Itani Lo Sahn.
- Indomitable will trigger if the player is dragged down by  Lash's  Ground Strike and  Billy's  Bashdown, but will not detach the player from their immobilize & pull effects, which continue until they reach the floor.

- While performing a similar effect,  Phantom Strike's pull does not trigger Indomitable.
- Indomitable will prevent players from being stunned after being parried.
- Indomitable will prevent players from being stunned after getting hit by bullets while on a zipline. They will still fall off the zipline.
- Players still receive health damage that gets applied before the barrier comes up from the following abilities and items:

- Graves -  Grasping Hands
- Grey Talon -   Guided Owl
- Haze -  Sleep Dagger
- Holliday -  Spirit Lasso
- Infernus -  Concussive Combustion
