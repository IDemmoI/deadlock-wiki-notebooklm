(I received this name for a reason. It must never be tarnished.)

Born to criminal royalty, Kaori and her brother Yamato always knew they were destined to run The 7th Moon after their father's passing.  For years they ran the organization as equals; leading to profit, success, and plans for expansion to the United States.

The idea was simple.  Kaori would stay in Japan and maintain things at home, while Yamato would travel to America to set up an international presence.  Unfortunately for the siblings; this plan didn't sit well with some of their more insecure underlings, who bristled at the idea of being led by Kaori in Japan.

The coup was swift and violent.  Yamato gave his life protecting Kaori, but in doing so ensured that she and a handful of loyalists were able to escape Japan for America.

When she arrived stateside, the contact her brother was supposed to meet greeted her and her retinue.  Unfamiliar with the gender of Japanese names, the man addressed her as Yamato.  She thought to correct him, but decided that the best way to keep her brother's memory alive was to take his name and live out his dream.

## Overview

[edit | edit source]

Yamato's wish in the ritual is to make the Seventh Moon powerful enough to take over New York.[1][2] She also hopes to solidify her brother's legacy, as she is still grieving his death.[3][4]

Yamato predominantly speaks Japanese, although she also knows how to speak English.

### Biography

[edit | edit source]

Yamato has arrived in New York less than a year before the Ritual.[5]

The sword Yamato uses is an ancient heirloom.[6] She inherited it when her predecessor died, and someone else will inherit it when she dies.[7]

### Personality

[edit | edit source]

Yamato is the ruthless leader of the Seventh Moon, and leads the organization by example.[8][9] She once murdered someone for owing the Seventh Moon a "C-Note" ($100).[10] She often attempts to threaten her teammates to get them to obey her.[11][12][13]

Yamato is cruel and antagonistic to most other heroes.[14] This generally goes unnoticed, since none of them speak Japanese, which she is aware of.[15]

Yamato is commonly described as "scary".[16]

### Information

[edit | edit source]

Yamato wants to make the OSIC and Nashala obey her.[17][18]

Yamato wants to speak to someone named "Molly Molenzo", a woman incarcerated at Rikers Island who got stabbed by  Vyper.[19][20]

## Character Relationships

[edit | edit source]

### Wraith

[edit | edit source]

Yamato views Wraith as her main hurdle towards conquering New York.[21] She hopes to take over Wraith's business.[22] While on the same team, Yamato intends to betray Wraith later.[23]

## Character Interactions

[edit | edit source]

- Yamato respects  Grey Talon for his age and skill.[24][25] She also acknowledges Apollo's potential.[26][27]
- Yamato wants to turn Mo into a coat and Krill into a hat.[28]
- Yamato likes Paige, likening her to a "puppy that pisses on the rug: stupid, yet strangely endearing".[29][30]
- Yamato thanks  Ivy for taking care of Mendoza.[31] However, she also undermines Ivy's skill and says she wants to take over Spanish Harlem for the Seventh Moon.[32][33]
- Yamato is offended by Victor and Viscous' existence.[34][35][36]
- Haze is keeping an eye on Yamato in-case the Seventh Moon becomes big enough to be a cartel. She hasn't been ordered to assassinate Yamato yet.[37][38]
- Pocket thinks Yamato would get along with their mother.[39]
- Grey Talon and Warden respect Yamato's conviction.[40][41]
- Graves really likes Yamato, even though she knows she shouldn't like her.[42][43][44]
- Lash,[45] Paradox[46] and Vyper[47] want Yamato's sword.
- Only the Patrons and  The Doorman know Yamato's true name is Kaori.[4][48]  Rem also learns Yamato's name if he sees her dreams.[49]

## Removed Lore

[edit | edit source]

In *Neon Prime,* Yamato had children.[50][51][52] Her father is also mentioned, and it is believed that he was the character Ghost/Spectre, who became  Lady Geist.

A popular fan rumor is that Yamato in *Neon Prime* was an alien obsessed with Japanese culture. This rumor is incorrect, based on what is known about her character and faction from old voice lines and the Neon Prime lore document.
