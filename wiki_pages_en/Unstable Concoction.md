**Unstable Concoction** is a Legendary  Spirit Item that can only be obtained in Street Brawl.

## Notes

[edit | edit source]

- Some abilities and items that bypass lethal damage can allow the player to survive Unstable Concotion, such as  Cheat Death and Shiv's  Bloodletting.
