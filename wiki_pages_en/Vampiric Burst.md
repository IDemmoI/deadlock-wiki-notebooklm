## Trivia

[edit | edit source]

- Vampiric Burst appears to have previously been named "Surging Power", as many hero voice lines for it are named "use_surging_power" and heroes will often say it when Vampiric Burst is activated.

- Additionally, Lash's in-game voice lines for Vampiric Burst are "lash_use_surging_power", while another set of unused lines named "lash_use_vampiric_burst" also exist.
- Venator's  Ira Domini and  Gutshot T3 heavy melee deal +5 damage to enemies with Vampiric Burst active.
