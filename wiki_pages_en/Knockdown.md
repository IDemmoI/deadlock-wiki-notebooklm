**Knockdown** is a Tier 3  Spirit Item that can be purchased from the Shop for  **3,200**.

- 1 Notes
- 2 Notable Interactions
- 3 Update history
- 4 Navigation

## Notes

[edit | edit source]

The bonus stun duration scales based on the target's height when hit, reaching its maximum +1.5s duration against targets 30m or higher in the air.

## Notable Interactions

[edit | edit source]

Knockdown is a strong counter to the following:

**Flying Heroes**

- Vindicta
- Grey Talon

**Channeled Abilities**

- Bebop —  Hyper Beam
- Dynamo —  Singularity
- Haze —  Bullet Dance
- Ivy —  Air Drop
- McGinnis —  Heavy Barrage
- Seven —  Storm Cloud
