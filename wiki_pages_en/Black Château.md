# Black Château

**The Black Château** is a mysterious location in *Deadlock*, frequented by academics.

## Summary

[edit | edit source]

The Black Château's mysteries were revealed to the general public for the first time in a New York Oracle article.[1]

Dynamo and  Kelvin last saw each other at the Black Château, before Kelvin's disappearance.[2]

Dynamo says "no one is leaving the Black Château" for  Lady Geist's gala event.[3]
