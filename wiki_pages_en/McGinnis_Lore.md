Maggie McGinnis always had a knack for building things.  At 4 she was using Lincoln Logs to recreate architectural marvels.  At 10 she built and rewired a custom light fixture for her parent's anniversary.  At 15 she designed a machine that refined the souls of the dead...that last one got a little more attention than the Lincoln Logs.

Now the head of Fairfax Industries Military R&D department, McGinnis spends her time in the machine shop, not in a lab.  She's not interested in theory, to her science is magic you can touch.

## Overview

[edit | edit source]

McGinnis' wish is unknown, although it's implied that she's using the ritual as a chance to test some weaponry.[1][2]

### Biography

[edit | edit source]

McGinnis' first name is Margaret, but she goes by Maggie.[3]

Maximilian Fairfax "paraded McGinnis incessantly" to the New York elite when he first discovered her as a genius teenage inventor.[4] McGinnis was famously known as a "kid genius".[5][6]

As the head engineer of Fairfax Industries Military R&D department, McGinnis brainstorms the occult war machines.[7][8] While it's unknown where McGinnis' inventions have been used, it's said that she may be resposible for millions of deaths.[9][10]

While McGinnis doesn't love working for Fairfax Industries, she's content with being a "wage slave" since she gets paid a fortune.[11][12] McGinnis was worried that she would lose her job when Fairfax lost the sovereignty of the Island of Cadenza to the US Government.[13][14]

In The Binding of Oathkeeper, McGinnis creates a new ward for Lady Geist to contain Oathkeeper with. McGinnis helps Lady Geist due to the chance to "harness the power of a God".[15]

### Personality

[edit | edit source]

As a "notorious misanthrope", McGinnis doesn't really care about the deaths she's caused or the sins of Fairfax Industries.[16][17][18] McGinnis thinks ethics are for scientists "that can't think big enough".[19] She does, however, appreciate the good  Ivy did for New York.[20]

### Information

[edit | edit source]

McGinnis is aware and makes it known that Spectral Wall usually backfires on her team.[21][22][23]

McGinnis' equipment and weaponry is powered by souls.[24][25]

McGinnis likes to drink beer.[26]

## Character Interactions

[edit | edit source]

- In *The Binding of Oathkeeper*, one possible storyline includes McGinnis building a new ward for  Lady Geist.
- McGinnis consistently mistakes Kelvin's name for "Kevin".[27][28] Most of McGinnis' pings for Kelvin include a variant where she calls him "Kevin".
- McGinnis accuses  Calico of stealing her ideas.[29]

- It's possible this is referring to Calico's old lore as a weapons dealer, as Calico used to use turrets while she was in Hero Labs.
- McGinnis wants to talk to  Shiv and "fix him".[30][31]
- McGinnis wants to sample  Viscous' goo.[32]
- McGinnis doesn't frequent  Infernus' bar, Jezebel's, because she finds it "too fancy". She does, however, want to go when Infernus offers her free drinks.[33]
- McGinnis is impressed by the craftsmanship used to create Bebop and shows interest in hiring his creator, Ms. Shelly.[34]
- McGinnis offers help to remove  Seven's headgear.[35]
- McGinnis offers to upgrade  Dynamo's body.[36]
- McGinnis appreciates what Ivy has done for New York.[37][38]
- McGinnis makes fun of Grey Talon and Infernus for not using guns.[39][40]
- Pocket loathes McGinnis due to her working for Fairfax Industries.[41][42][43]
- Seven criticizes McGinnis for her lack of creativity.[44]
- The Doorman calls McGinnis "the brightest mind humanity has to offer".[45]
- Apollo's father is a "fan" of McGinnis.[46]
- Mina and Wraith call McGinnis "Fairfax's pet genius".[47][48]
- Paradox is interested in McGinnis' research and the secrets she holds for Fairfax.[49][50] McGinnis has been trying to keep Paradox out of her lab.[51]
