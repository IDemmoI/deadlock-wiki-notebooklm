**Vortex Web** is a Tier 4  Spirit Item that can be purchased from the Shop for  **6,400**. Its component,  Slowing Hex, makes up  **1,600** of the cost.

## Notes

[edit | edit source]

- Vortex Web can be cast at a target enemy player, pulling other enemies towards them.
- The Vortex Web model is an octopus that pulls players with its arms.
