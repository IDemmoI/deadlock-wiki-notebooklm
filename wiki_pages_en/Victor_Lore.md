A lot of people spend their whole lives wondering who they really are. For Victor, this existential question is made more complicated as he woke up on a mortuary table with no knowledge of his creator, why he was made, and whom he is made of.

Now searching the five boroughs for answers, Victor is hoping to find someone who can identify any part of him.

## Overview

[edit | edit source]

### Biography

[edit | edit source]

Victor is made out of dead body parts.[1] Though he has been searching for a while, he has yet to find the origins of any of his body parts.[2]

### Personality

[edit | edit source]

Victor's in constant chronic pain, with the only relief being from  The Cube.[3] He's often seen by the other heroes as sad or pitiable.[4]

Victor's willing to do anything for answers on his origins.[5]

### Information

[edit | edit source]

Because Victor doesn't know where pieces of his body come from, he hasn't replaced or discarded any limbs.[6] It's implied that Victor's physical condition is deteriorating, and his left arm keeps falling off.[7][6]

## Character Interactions

[edit | edit source]

- Victor is considered attractive. Infernus calls him "a pretty good looking corpse",[8] and Silver refers to him as the "prettiest corpse on Manhattan".[9] Paige tells Victor never to put on a shirt,[10] and Mina offers to properly sew his body together if he'll work as a clothing model at one of her shows.[11]

- Ivy attempts to ask Victor out to dinner.[12]
- Victor often asks other characters for any leads on his origins. He asks Mo & Krill if he can search the New York sewers to see if any body parts down there match his own.[13] He also asks Dynamo where Columbia College gets their cadavers for their medical program.[14] Victor asks Abrams if any of his past cases included "a mad genius who lost one of his creations".[15]
- Victor asks Abrams for help with uncovering his past.[16]
- Victor wishes Pocket luck with their problems.[17]
- Victor and Kelvin promised each other that they'd stick together, as they're both walking corpses.[18][19]
- Lady Geist suggests that Victor should see her grandson, Johann Jr., to get him fixed.[7]

- Lady Geist calls Johann her son. It's unclear if this is a mistake or a lie by Lady Geist to hide her age.
- Mina offers to stitch up Victor's wounds if he stars in one of her fashion shows.[20]
- Infernus and Kelvin tell Victor to focus on his future instead of his past.[21][22]
- Ivy and Paige have offered to help Victor with his problems.[23][24]
- Bebop, Grey Talon, Kelvin, Krill, Mirage and Vindicta wish Victor luck in uncovering his past.[4]
- Seven believes Victor doesn't know a thing about "real" suffering.[25]
- Yamato is offended by Victor's existence.[26][27]
- Bebop has invited Victor to see Ms. Shelly.[28]
- The Doorman has invited Victor to the Baroness Hotel.[29]
