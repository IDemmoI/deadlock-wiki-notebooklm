Like most teenagers; Infernus was wild, rebellious, and impetuous.  Unlike most teenagers, Infernus was a creature from another plane and had a supernatural mastery over fire.  Needless to say:  his youth was filled with no small amount of arson, murder, and evidence disposal.  But that was then.  Now an adult, Infernus has mellowed out considerably.  He’s happy working at a bar with good live music, and talking to interesting people.  That being said when someone at the bar gets belligerent or violent, he’s not afraid to dust off the skills he once honed.

## Overview

[edit | edit source]

Infernus' motivation in the ritual is to prevent Jezebel's Billiards Hall, the bar he tends to, from getting shut down down.[1][2]

### Biography

[edit | edit source]

As a second generation Ixian immigrant,[3] Infernus was born and raised in New York and has never visited Ixia.[4] He considers New York his home and doesn't follow news about Ixia or the Ixian war.[5]

Infernus doesn't know how to speak Ixian because his parents only ever spoke English around him.[6]

Infernus used to work for the Mendoza Syndicate before he got replaced by  Vyper.[7] It's implied that he killed people at "Pier 31" during his youth.[8] When he was roughly 18 years old, he "needed help, and someone gave it to him",[9] likely referring to Hank saving his life and allowing him to leave his life of crime behind.[10]

Infernus' visual novel will tell of the story of Infernus trying to save Jezebel's.

### Personality

[edit | edit source]

Infernus is haunted by his past and worries that he'll enjoy the thrills of the ritual and potentially fall back into a life of crime.[11][12][13]

### Information

[edit | edit source]

Infernus claims to make the best sidecar in Manhattan.[14]

Infernus is always well-dressed.[15]

Infernus wants to watch Cadence's set after the ritual.[16]

## Character Relationships

[edit | edit source]

### Hank

[edit | edit source]

Hank is Infernus' boss and friend at Jezebel's.

Infernus mainly wants to save the bar for Hank's sake because Hank "saved his life".[17][18] As mentioned in the biography, Hank likely helped Infernus leave his life of crime behind.[9][10]

Since Infernus doesn't want Hank to worry about him, he hasn't told Hank about his involvement with the ritual.[19]

### Wraith

[edit | edit source]

Wraith and Infernus used to work together while Infernus was a criminal.[20] While on the same team, Wraith tries to coax Infernus back into his criminal past.[21][22]

Wraith believes Infernus' work as a bartender is "boring". She also believes Infernus misses her, but he denies it.[23]

Infernus plays in Wraith's weekly poker game.[24] However, he tells  Mo & Krill that he wants to stay away from Wraith out of fear of her pulling him back into crime.[25]

According to  Graves, Wraith told Blackmore Academy students that play cards with her about Infernus' criminal past "with the mob".[26]

### Lady Geist

[edit | edit source]

As someone who frequents Jezebel's, Lady Geist is friends with Infernus and Hank.[27]

Lady Geist refers to Infernus as "Fern" and "her bartender".[28][29] She likes Infernus' recipe of gin and tonic.[30]

As Jezebel's is under threat of closure, Lady Geist offers to help Infernus save the bar.[31] In one pathway in *The Binding of Oathkeeper,* Lady Geist gives Oathkeeper to Hank on the promise of providing the resources needed to keep the bar open.[32]

### Mo & Krill

[edit | edit source]

Infernus is friends with Mo and Krill.[33]

Mo & Krill are regular patrons at Jezebel's, and offer to help Infernus prevent Jezebel's from closing.[34]

### Celeste

[edit | edit source]

Infernus is friends with Celeste and her found family. Celeste invites Infernus to come over to say hi to her family at the sideshow.[35]

Celeste refers to Infernus as "Fern".[36] She's also familiar with Hank and asks Infernus how Hank is holding up.[37]

### Mina

[edit | edit source]

Infernus once kicked Mina out of Jezebel's for using a fake ID on her 16th birthday.[38] She attempted to bribe him with money, to which he declined.[39]

### The Doorman

[edit | edit source]

Infernus and The Doorman respect each other for being members of the service industry and are apologetic when fighting each other.[40][41][42]

### Infernus' other patrons

[edit | edit source]

As a bartender, Infernus is familiar with a lot of characters who frequent Jezebel's.

These characters include but aren't limited to:   Abrams,[43]  Kelvin,[44]  Lash,[45] ,  Pocket,[46] the Shopkeeper,[47]  Shiv,[48] and  Silver.[49]

## Character Interactions

[edit | edit source]

- Infernus seems to know Ms. Shelly, even though they've never met before.[50][51]
- Infernus invites Holliday for a "date" at Jezebel's.[52]
- Infernus knows Paige's parents and is apologetic about her brother, Bryce.[53][54]
- Infernus has invited  Holliday,  McGinnis and Viscous to Jezebel's.[52][55][56]
- Infernus' friends and regular patrons, like  Abrams,[57] Krill,[58]  Lady Geist,[59] Shiv,[60]  the Shopkeeper,[61]Victor[62] and  Wraith[63] call him **Fern**.
- Apollo is shocked that Infernus doesn't speak Ixian nor care for Ixian culture.[64][65]
- Seven,  Vyper and  Wraith tease Infernus for leaving his criminal life behind and becoming a bartender.[66][67][23]
- Grey Talon is remorseful when fighting Infernus, due to his feelings towards Ixians.[68][69]
