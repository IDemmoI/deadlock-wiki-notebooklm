**Fortitude** is a Tier 3  Vitality Item that can be purchased from the Shop for  **3,200**. Its component,  Extra Health, makes up  **800** of the cost.

When the Fortitude buff is active, it appears as a glowing orange Ouroboros symbol in the player's back.
