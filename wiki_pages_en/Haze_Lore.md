The Occult Security and Investigation Commission is a black box government organization that makes the CIA look transparent.  It falls on the men, women, and entities of the OSIC to keep the country safe from sinister forces that are beyond the capabilities of local law enforcement.

As a member of the notorious Sandman Division, Haze is routinely tasked with infiltrating the dreams of persons of interest, to assess their threat level, and if needed... to put them to sleep.

## Overview

[edit | edit source]

Haze is fighting in the ritual on behalf of the OSIC.[1][2] Her wish is unknown, although the Patrons tempt her to wish herself a new life.[3][4][5]

### Biography

[edit | edit source]

Haze has prepared extensively for the ritual by performing drills, research, stretching, divination, and fortune telling.[6]

### Personality

[edit | edit source]

Haze works for the OSIC because she "loves her country".[7] She acknowledges that the OSIC is flawed, but in her eyes the ends justify the means.[8] She is extremely loyal to her superiors and her job,[9][10][11] although it's implied that she doesn't love being a Sandman.[12][13]

### Information

[edit | edit source]

Haze is nicknamed "Dreamwalker" and "Dreamstalker".[14]

Haze appreciates quality knifework.[15]

Haze plays the cello for fun.[16]

## Character Relationships

[edit | edit source]

### Agent Marlowe

[edit | edit source]

Agent Marlowe is implied to be one of Haze's superiors.[17][18]

Haze believes Marlowe would want to know about  The Doorman's involvement in the ritual.[19]

### Rem

[edit | edit source]

Haze and Rem are friends.[20][21][22] Haze promised to help Rem get home and dislikes him fighting in the ritual due to the dangers.[23][24]

Rem calls Haze his "mom".[25]

Rem sees Haze when he sleeps and claims that "she's him".[26][27]

Rem seems to have caused Haze some trouble prior to the ritual,[28] but how the two met and how they know each other is currently unknown.

### Grey Talon

[edit | edit source]

Haze and Grey Talon know each other. She refers to him by his first name and sent a card when his son died.[29]

They haven't seen each other for "a while".[30]

### Viscous

[edit | edit source]

Haze takes Viscous and his problems seriously.[31]

Haze wants to know more about The Adversary in case more creatures like it come through The Deep's astral gate.[32]

## Character Interactions

[edit | edit source]

- Most characters dislike fighting Haze, since nobody wants to piss off the OSIC.[33]
- Haze suggests a position at the OSIC to  Abrams,[34] Graves,[35] Victor[36] and Vindicta.[37]
- Haze is worried about Apollo participating in the ritual since him dying would cause an international incident.[38][39]
- Haze appreciates what Ivy did for New York.[40] She doesn't think Ivy would be a good OSIC agent, however, due to Ivy's big heart.[41]
- Haze tells  Dynamo that OSIC agent Marlowe has an offer for Dynamo to join them. Dynamo mistakes Marlowe for his wife, Marla.[42]
- Haze has been ordered to assassinate Calico and Seven.[43][44] She's also keeping an eye on The Doorman,[45] Lady Geist,[46] Wraith[47] and Yamato,[48] but hasn't been told to kill them since they aren't considered national threats yet. The OSIC aren't aware of Vyper and Haze views her as "beneath her paygrade".[49][50]
- Haze and Mirage respect each other's discipline.[51][52]
- Haze views Wraith as "a worthy target".[53]
- When on the same team,  Seven teases Haze for working alongside him and asks how soon will they start fighting each other once the ritual is over.[54][55]

## Removed Lore

[edit | edit source]

Haze (Neon Prime)

### Removed character interactions

[edit | edit source]

Haze originally had kill lines implying that she helped  Infernus walk free after his criminal youth.[56][57] These lines were removed in the Six New Heroes Update.

### Neon Prime

[edit | edit source]

In *Neon Prime,* Haze's design was an alien with long ears. She was a member of the Disciples of Kreela, a faction that worshipped a "Goddess of Assassination".[58]
