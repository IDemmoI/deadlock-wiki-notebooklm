# Soul Rebirth

“Farewell, my friends.”

This page contains game content that has been removed from the game.

**Soul Rebirth** was a Tier 4 Vitality Item that provided Spirit Power to the purchasing hero and increased basic respawn time.

**Soul Rebirth** had a Passive component which would respawn the hero with 50% health at the location they died after 4 seconds; this effect had a cooldown of 180 seconds, which could be reduced with additional Cooldown Reduction.

On November 7, 2024, this item was removed from the game.

## Notes

[edit | edit source]

- When **Soul Rebirth**'s passive effect triggers, an animation is shown of the hero rising from their body before finally respawning.
- Killing a hero that has **Soul Rebirth** ready will still give kill credit and reward the instant souls, but the hero's corpse will not release additional soul orbs.
- Killing a hero that has **Soul Rebirth** ready will trigger on-kill effects, such as  Healbane's passive.
