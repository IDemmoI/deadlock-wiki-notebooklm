## Hero abilities removed by Dispel Magic

[edit | edit source]

The following debuffs can be removed at any moment:

- Bebop -  Sticky Bomb (bomb is dropped on the ground) and  Grapple Arm (Tier 1)
- Celeste -  Light Eater,  Dazzling Trick and  Radiant Daggers
- The Doorman -  Call Bell (Movement Slow) and  Luggage Cart
- Drifter -  Stalker's Mark
- Dynamo -  Kinetic Pulse (Tier 2)
- Graves -  Grasping Hands and  Essence Theft
- Grey Talon -  Spirit Snare (Tier 2 penalty only)
- Haze -  Sleep Dagger (Tier 1 and  tier 3 penalty only) and  Fixation
- Infernus -  Napalm and  Afterburn
- Ivy -  Entangling Thorns and  Air Drop (Tier 2)
- Kelvin -  Frost Grenade
- Lady Geist -  Life Drain and  Malice
- Lash -  Flog (Tier 1 and tier 2)
- McGinnis -  Spectral Wall (Movement Slow and T1)
- Mina -  Love Bites
- Mirage -  Fire Scarabs and  Djinn's Mark
- Mo & Krill -  Scorn (Tier 3) and  Sand Blast
- Paige -  Captivating Read
- Paradox -  Pulse Grenade,  Time Wall, and  Kinetic Carbine
- Pocket -  Enchanter's Satchel (Tier 3)
- Seven -  Static Charge and  Power Surge (Tier 3)
- Shiv -  Serrated Knives
- Silver -  Boot Kick,  Entangling Bola,  Mauling Leap and  Tail Whack
- Sinclair -  Vexing Bolt (Tier 1)
- Venator -  Consecrating Grenade and  Hex-Lined Snap Trap
- Vindicta -  Stake and  Crow Familiar
- Viscous -  Puddle Punch (Movement Slow)
- Vyper -  Screwjab Dagger and  Lethal Venom
- Warden -  Alchemical Flask and  Binding Word
- Yamato -  Power Slash (Tier 1) and  Crimson Slash

Additionally, displacement (knockup) debuffs can be removed if the item is activated at the moment of impact:

- Billy's  Bashdown
- Bebop's  Exploding Uppercut
- Dynamo's  Kinetic Pulse
- Holliday's  Powder Keg
- Lash's  Ground Strike (Tier 2)
