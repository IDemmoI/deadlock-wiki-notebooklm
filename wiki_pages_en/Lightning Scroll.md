**Lightning Scroll** is a Tier 4  Spirit Item that can be purchased from the Shop for  **6,400**. Its component,  Mystic Slow, makes up  **1,600** of the cost.

## Notes

[edit | edit source]

- The "**Mystic Slow effects**" listed in the tooltip include the -12% Dash Distance Reduction, though it's not directly listed in the item description. This is mentioned in the January 30, 2026 patch notes.
- Drifter,  Kelvin,  Mirage,  Pocket, and  Yamato cannot buy Lightning Scroll, as their ultimates cannot trigger its passive effect.

- Lady Geist's ultimate does not trigger Lightning Scroll, though she can still buy it. This may be an unintended interaction of Geist's ult only dealing Pure Damage. (However, Lightning Scroll *does* trigger for ultimates that deal Pure Damage on a low health threshold, such as  Shiv's  Killing Blow, if the player survives with  Cheat Death or similar effect).
- Damage dealt by  Silver's Weapon or Abilities while transformed will trigger Lightning Scroll.
