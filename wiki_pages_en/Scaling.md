# Scaling

**Scaling** refers to Stats or properties of Abilities and Items that increase incrementally based on another stat.

## Overview

[edit | edit source]

Scaling is represented by a number next to the scaling icon, which indicates how much the stat is multiplied by. The scaling value is added to the stat's base value. The player can see how much value is added by the scaling by holding Tab or Alt.

Most scaling properties have effects based on the stat they scale from. However, there may be exceptions such as  Paradox's  Kinetic Carbine, which scales off Weapon Damage but deals Spirit Damage.

## List of scaling types

[edit | edit source]

- Boon scaling
- Spirit Power scaling
- Weapon Damage scaling
- Melee Damage scaling
