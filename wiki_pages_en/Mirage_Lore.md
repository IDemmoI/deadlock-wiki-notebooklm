The Djinn are one of the more curious groups of people in the modern world.  Possessing great power in controlled doses, Djinn are incapable of maintaining corporeal form for longer than 48 minutes without resting within a vessel.  As a result, most Djinn employ human bodyguards for transport, protection, and companionship.

Mirage is one such bodyguard.  Employed by Djinn ambassador Nashala Dion, Mirage is in New York on a diplomatic mission.  It's the hope of the Djinn that they can negotiate with the US government for a sale of part of Wyoming so that they can build a sovereign nation for their people.

## Overview

[edit | edit source]

Mirage's wish (which is moreso Nashala's wish) is to help Nashala buy a piece of Wyoming to turn into a home for the Djinn.[1][2][3]

### Biography

[edit | edit source]

Mirage has worked for Nashala for a few years, after she approached him with a job offer.[4]

Mirage and Nashala haven't had the chance to do much sightseeing, but are looking forward to seeing various locales,[5][6] such as New Jersey and Columbia University.[7][8]

While in New York, Mirage and Nashala are guests at the Baroness Hotel.[9][10]

### Personality

[edit | edit source]

Mirage views the Djinn as "people". He thinks some are "fascinating", but some others are "assholes".[11]

Mirage likes to play bowling and calls it "the sport of kings". He learned it in England.[12]

Most characters respect Mirage due to his conviction for the Djinn.[13]

### Information

[edit | edit source]

Mirage has visited New York's Public Library.[6]

Mirage wants to try eating a hot dog.[14]

Mirage dislikes the Paradox Organisation, since they keep trying to steal Nashala for the Paradox Museum.[15][16][17]

## Character Relationships

[edit | edit source]

### Nashala Dion

[edit | edit source]

Mirage is deeply loyal to Nashala and the Djinn's plight,[18][19] willing to die and kill for her.[20]

While Mirage enjoys Nashala's company, he can find her frustrating to work with when she makes "impulsive, short-sighted" decisions (such as asking him to trust  Seven).[21]

Mirage fights with Nashala by his side since he believes that's the safest place for her.[22]

Anyone who wants to talk to Nashala must have Mirage's approval,[23] and anyone trying to steal or harm Nashala will be killed by Mirage.[24][25]

Mirage believes that Nashala doesn't need a wish to pull off the deal,[26] since the Djinn can offer America money and power.[27]

### The Doorman

[edit | edit source]

Mirage and The Doorman are familiar with each other from the Baroness Hotel. Both are apologetic when killing each other.[28][29]

## Character Interactions

[edit | edit source]

- Mirage asks  Dynamo for a tour of Columbia University.[30]
- Mirage calls out Abrams and Calico for valuing money over duty.[31][32]
- Mirage shows no mercy towards monsters like  Drifter,[33]  Lady Geist,[34] and  Seven.[35]
- Mina, Silver and  Vyper want Mirage to "stop yelling about the Djinn".[36][37][38]

- Billy likens Mirage to 'people who think having a dog is like having a personality' and also wants him to stop talking about the Djinn.[39]
- Paige admires Mirage and says he "stepped out of every romance novel ever written".[40][41] She thinks Mirage is better than  Shiv.[42]
- Lady Geist,  Mina and  Seven respect Mirage for "knowing his place".[43][44][45]
- Paradox and  Vyper think Mirage needs to "lighten up".[46][47]
- Billy and  Drifter think Mirage is handsome.[48][49]
