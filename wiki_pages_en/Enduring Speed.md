**Enduring Speed** is a Tier 2  Vitality Item that can be purchased from the Shop for  **1,600**. Its component,  Sprint Boots, makes up  **800** of the cost.

It is a component of  Juggernaut.

## Sources of Slow

[edit | edit source]

The item makes any slow applications 25% less potent. For example an ability that applies 60% Move Slow, when affecting a hero with Enduring Speed will reduce their speed only by 45%.
