# Damage Barrier

“Cannot waste time.”

**Notes:** The list of hero abilities is a bit outdated after the March 6 2026 update

Pocket with 600 HP barrier

**Barriers** are a temporary pool of additional health that takes damage before Health is reduced. Barriers block all incoming damage from any source, with Damage Resistances applied.

## Notes

[edit | edit source]

- Any instance of excess damage dealt to completely destroy a barrier is then done to health.

- For example, a Dynamo with 100 barrier health and 0% Spirit Resist takes 168 spirit damage from Vindicta's Assassinate. 100 spirit damage will break Dynamo's barrier, the remaining 68 spirit damage is done to health.
