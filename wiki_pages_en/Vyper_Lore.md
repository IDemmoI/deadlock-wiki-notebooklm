## Overview

[edit | edit source]

Vyper's wish seems to be a new and secure life for herself.[1][2][3]

### Biography

[edit | edit source]

Vyper is a gorgon.[4][5] She often refers to herself as "V".[6][7]

Vyper has been to New Jersey,[8] Staten Island,[9] Philadelphia,[10] Illinois,[11] Oklahoma[12] and Wisconsin.[13] She has been incarcerated (and escaped from) Riker's Island multiple times.[14] She doesn't want to be sent to Lost Whisper.[15]

Vyper used to work for the Mendoza Syndicate, acting as Infernus' replacement.[16][17] When working for Mendoza, Vyper was caught by Ivy and tried for 13 counts of murder, although Vyper mentions that the actual kill count was way higher.[18][19] Vyper has killed tourists in the past and felt no remorse for doing so.[20]

Vyper says her mother was "slinging poison" after she found out about a life insurance policy, implying that she murdered her spouse for the insurance.[21] Similarly, Vyper wants to get married to a rich husband and kill him for the inheritance.[22]

### Personality

[edit | edit source]

Vyper calls herself a "degenerate"[23] and has "real bad impulse control".[24]

### Information

[edit | edit source]

Vyper has been kicked out of the Paradox Museum and the Baroness Hotel.[25][26] She's also been barred from Jezebel's.[27] Vyper once crashed Fashion Week.[28]

Vyper makes references to pruno, an improvised alcoholic beverage also known as "prison wine".[29][30]

Vyper has a Midwest accent, according to Mina.[31] Though according to Vyper's voice actress Stephanie Kerbis, Vyper is "not from Wisconsin or New Jersey".[32]

## Character Relationships

[edit | edit source]

### Billy

[edit | edit source]

Billy and Vyper are familiar with each other. While on the same team, Billy and Vyper flirt with each other. Vyper asks if she can call Billy "Lash", to which Billy doesn't care.[33]

While Vyper holds Billy in high regard,[34][35] Billy thinks Vyper is a moron.[36][37]

It's implied that Vyper knew Billy from before he got his goat head.[38]

### Lash

[edit | edit source]

Vyper is infatuated with Lash.[39][40][41] She has watched him sleep (without his knowledge)[42] and sends Lash letters made to "stand out" (the contents of the letters are unknown, but they do make Lash uncomfortable).[43][44] When Vyper flirts with  Billy, she asks if she can call him "Lash".[33]

Lash is scared of Vyper's obession with him and wants her to leave him alone.[45][46][47]

### Silver

[edit | edit source]

Silver has hunted and caught Vyper so many times that Vyper's use of  Petrifying Bola comes from Silver's  Entangling Bola.[14][48]

### Wraith

[edit | edit source]

Wraith wants Vyper dead since she once robbed one of Wraith's poker rooms.[49][50] She also doesn't allow Vyper to take out markers (informal loans) since Vyper never pays them back.[51][52] Vyper knows Sophia Arroyo works for Wraith.[53]

## Character Interactions

[edit | edit source]

- Nobody trusts Vyper,[54] with the exception of Rem.[55][56]
- Vyper dislikes  Bebop, Lash's pitfighting rival.[57]
- Aside from Lash, Vyper is attracted to Abrams,[58][59] Billy[33] and Mirage.[60] She also likes Cinnamon, a "librarian" from "Pole Palace" who resembles Paige.[61]
- Vyper likes  Mo & Krill.[62] They don't like her.[63]
- Vyper believes  Infernus "used to be hard, but now he's just a bitch".[64] She also liked Shiv before he went straight.[65][66]
- Vyper says her Screwjab Daggers are better than  Shiv's Serrated Knives.[67]
- Vyper says  Calico has a reputation of "sloppy work" due to "a hit that went bad" (a possible reference to the attempted assassination of  Pocket).[68]
- Vyper nicknames Paige "Red" and knew her brother, Bryce.[69][70]
- Vyper comments on  Grey Talon's health, due to his age.[71]
- Vyper wants  Mirage to "stop yelling about the Djinn".[72]
- Vyper is interested in stealing Ava,[73] Graves' lich hand,[74] Kelvin's parka,[75] Lady Geist's earrings,[76] Pocket's suitcase[77] and Yamato's sword.[78] She's robbed the Shopkeeper before.[79]
- Haze mentions that eliminating cretins like Vyper is beneath her pay grade.[80]
- Ivy is familar with Vyper from when she worked for Mendoza.[81][18][19]
