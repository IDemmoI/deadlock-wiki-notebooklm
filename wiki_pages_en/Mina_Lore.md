Summoning the Patrons never looked this good.

Mina Ha had it all...rich parents, elite education, and an arranged marriage that would see her ascend from run-of-the-mill upper class to American royalty.  She was happy.

Then her betrothed was assassinated.

Mina wept; not for the life of her fiancé, but for the future that was stolen from her.  Why couldn’t the killer just wait until after the wedding? Then she’d have the last name she always dreamed of and no one would even have to pretend to love anyone.  It could have been perfect, but life wasn’t fair...and so Mina did what she always does when things don’t go her way: she told her parents to fix it.

Mr. And Mrs. Ha knew that the only way to appease their daughter was to offer her something greater than what she lost... and marrying into one of the world’s most lauded families was a tall order to top.  They had to think big.  They had to think outside of the box.  They had to promise their daughter immortality.

It took a small fortune, but eventually they found a vampire who was willing to turn her.  Unsurprisingly, Mina thought the idea of eternal youth and a trust fund was an acceptable plan B and happily agreed to the proposal.  Now a fledgling vampire, Mina is quickly discovering that the clout she held as a human has no weight in vampire high society...and while at first that annoyed her, if there is one thing Mina has a knack for, it's learning the rules so she can subvert them.  She will be the Viscount of New York, the other vampires just don't know it yet.

## Overview

[edit | edit source]

Mina's motivation in the ritual is to become Viscount of New York.[1]

### Biography

[edit | edit source]

Mina graduated from Blackmore Academy.[2]

Mina's fiancé was  Pocket,[3][4][5] who suffered an assassination attempt by  Calico 5 years before the events of the game.[6][7]

Mina is a fashion designer and owns a clothing brand.[8][9] She knows how to sew.[10]

### Personality

[edit | edit source]

Mina is generally antagonistic to most other characters.[11] According to  Vindicta, Mina has a reputation for being unpleasant.[12]

### Information

[edit | edit source]

Mina complains that her parents "botched" her turning into a vampire.[13]

## Character Relationships

[edit | edit source]

### Pocket

[edit | edit source]

As mentioned in the biography, Mina's fiancé was Pocket.[3][4][5]

Mina hates Pocket for "ruining her life", as Pocket's assassination meant Mina couldn't become a Fairfax.[14][15]

### Lady Geist

[edit | edit source]

Mina idolises Lady Geist,[16][17] who believes Mina has "potential".[18] Mina wants to dress Geist for the Met Gala.[19]

### Infernus

[edit | edit source]

Mina got carded at Jezebel's by Infernus on her 16th birthday and attempted to bribe him to let her stay.[20][21]

## Character Interactions

[edit | edit source]

- Mina idolises Lady Geist,[16][17] who believes Mina has "potential".[18] Mina wants to dress Geist for the Met Gala.[19]
- Mina offers to stitch up Victor's wounds if he models at one of her shows.[22]
- Mina is terrified of Drifter.[23][24] Drifter thinks Mina is a "pathetic excuse for a vampire".[25]
- Mina thinks Billy might have rabies.[26]
- As a fashion designer, Mina dislikes Abrams' coat,[27] Dynamo's jacket,[28] The Doorman's hat,[29] Vindicta's outfit,[30] and calls Paige a "walking fashion crime".[31] She does, however, like Haze's uniform and tie,[32][33] Holliday's hat,[34] Lady Geist's everything,[35]Victor's leather pants[36] and Wraith's hat.[37]
- Mina knows someone named Sandra who thinks Lash is hot.[38]
- Mo & Krill allegedly sold knock-offs of Mina's dresses.[39] Krill thinks Mina's fashion line is "overwrought".[40]
