## Abilities

[edit | edit source]

Druid has no working abilities or upgrades. However, three of Druid's abilities are named:

- **"Plant_Obscure_Bush"*.*** This ability has a 15 second cooldown.
- **"Plant_Branch_Wall"**. This likely uses the "shrub wall" model in the files. It has a 10 second cooldown.
- **"Sprout"**. Includes a prompt to be used on allies, although cannot be used on self. It has a 1 second cooldown.

There is no information for Druid's ultimate.
