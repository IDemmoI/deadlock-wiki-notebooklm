## Overview

[edit | edit source]

Ivy has a few motivations for being in the ritual. She wants to keep Spanish Harlem safe,[1] make life better for the Arroyos,[2][3] find her new purpose in life now that the Mendoza Syndicate has been dismantled,[4][5] help Carlos Arroyo secure his place as New York's mayor,[6][7] and (if she finds out about Sophia's job as  Wraith's bookmaker)[8][9] keep Sophia out of trouble.[10]

### Biography

[edit | edit source]

Ivy has been alive for 20 years.[11] She doesn't know who created her, but she doesn't care since "her life started when she met the Arroyos".[12]

Ivy's visual novel will show her efforts to get a job and support the Arroyos.

### Personality

[edit | edit source]

Ivy is kind and friendly to others. She dislikes organized crime due to her history with Mendoza.[13][14] She prefers to fight crime without resorting to killing.[15]

### Information

[edit | edit source]

Ivy has only recently started leaving Spanish Harlem, and is looking forward to sightseeing.[16] She wants to watch South Pacific.[17]

Ivy is referred to as "the savior of Spanish Harlem",[18][19] but she doesn't like the nickname.[20]

Ivy's efforts to save Spanish Harlem were reported by news in Illinois, due to public interest.[21]

## Character Relationships

[edit | edit source]

### The Arroyos

[edit | edit source]

Ivy's sister, Sophia Arroyo (or Sophie), is studying at university under Dynamo,[22] and works with  Wraith to pay for her tuition.[9] Carlos Arroyo is a 1st-grade teacher[23] who's running an election campaign for mayor of New York City.[24][7] Carlos' wife is named Maria.[25] Ivy also has a little brother named Manny,[26] who's a fan of Kelvin.[27] The Arroyos are implied to be Catholic.[28]

Ivy is reluctant to leave the Arroyos on their own for too long.[29][30] This could be due to the fear that the Arroyos will be hurt if she's away from them for too long.[31][32][33]

### Dynamo

[edit | edit source]

With Sophia being a university student under Dynamo, Ivy worries her fighting Dynamo during the ritual would preconceive him against Sophia.[34] Dynamo reaffirms Ivy that Sophia's grades won't be affected by Ivy's actions in the ritual.[35] He warns Ivy that Sophia fell asleep during class due to her job, which Ivy didn't know about.[36]

### Lash

[edit | edit source]

Lash likes Ivy due to Mendoza's hatred towards both of them.[37][38] In one of the storylines in Ivy's Visual Novel, he helps her go after Mendoza.

### Vyper

[edit | edit source]

Ivy knows Vyper from when Vyper used to work for Mendoza.[39][40][41] Vyper knows that Sophia is working for Wraith, but doesn't want to get involved in "family drama".[8]

### Wraith

[edit | edit source]

Ivy doesn't trust Wraith and warns her to stay out of Spanish Harlem.[13][14] In one of the storylines in Ivy's Visual Novel, Ivy finds out that Sophia is working for Wraith.[9]

## Character Interactions

[edit | edit source]

- Most characters like Ivy and are apologetic when fighting her.[42]
- Ivy is naturally distrustful towards other criminals, especially  Wraith and  Yamato,[43] who are both leaders of their own criminal organizations.
- Ivy knows Paige from Manny Arroyo's story time.[44] She likes Paige and wants to start a book club with her.[45][46]
- Ivy invites Bebop and Ms. Shelly over to visit the Arroyos.[47]
- Ivy expresses admiration for  Shiv[48] and calls him a "shampoo ad with knives".[49]
- Billy believes that Ivy is a stooge who was used by Carlos Arroyo to become mayor.[50][51]
- Haze appreciates what Ivy did for New York, but believes she wouldn't be a good OSIC agent due to her big heart.[52][53]
- Seven respects Ivy for fighting for her ideals.[54]
- Apollo respects Ivy but believes she shouldn't have saved Spanish Harlem, instead believing that they should've saved themselves rather than rely solely on Ivy.[55][56]
- Vindicta believes Ivy is naive and warns her that, if she doesn't smarten up soon, someone she cares about will be hurt.[15]
- Warden respects Ivy,[57] but he also believes she's "meddling in things she doesn't understand".[58]
